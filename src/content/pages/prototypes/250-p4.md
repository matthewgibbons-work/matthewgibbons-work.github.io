# P4 · Responsive + Forms

## In short

Build a page with a form for people who want to send you something: a form anyone can fill in, on any screen down to 320px, that lands them on a thank-you page. Then make it quick to fill in on a phone with one thumb, and write up what you tried in `NOTES.md`.

## The problem

Most people who fill in a small site's form do it on a phone, in a hurry. Most first drafts fail them: the labels are grey placeholders that vanish as soon as they type, a row of radio buttons has no question above it, every field is red before they've touched it, an error shows only as a red border, the form is wider than the phone, and the Submit button is too small to hit.

S08 solved the screen part: one column on a phone, more when there's room, nothing pushing the page sideways. The Forms unit (Wed Nov 18) gives you the form: labels, groups, HTML's own checks, and errors in words. P4 puts them together. The next step adds a feature most forms never get: **make it quick to fill in on a phone, with one thumb.**

**Like P3, it has a required next step** (below) that adds a feature beyond the first problem. Trying it and writing up what happened is part of Meets; getting it working is a way to reach Exceeds.

It's due Tue Nov 24, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Rework until Dec 9.

It's your last Prototype: bring questions to class on Mon Nov 23, and once it's in, your studio time goes to the Final Project, where a form like this one is a good page to reuse. The step most people miss is the error messages: use `:user-invalid`, not `:invalid` (which turns every field red before anyone types), and say what's wrong in words, not only in red.

## The brief

The page has a short intro, the form, and a panel that says what happens next. When someone submits, they land on a thank-you page in the same folder. GitHub Pages can't store the answers, and that's fine: the work is in a form anyone can fill in, on any screen. On a phone, it's one column: intro, form, panel. When there's room, the form and the panel sit side by side. Pick who's sending what:

- the **Saturday Morning Archive**: suggest a cartoon for the archive,
- a farmers market: apply for a stall,
- a trail guide: report trail conditions after a hike,
- a campus club: sign up for the club fair,
- a band: join the waitlist for a sold-out show,
- or your own idea, for a real or realistic audience. Say who in your Intent.

## Next step: add a feature

Your visitor is filling this in on a bus, with one thumb. **How can every field bring up the right keyboard, fill itself in where the phone already knows the answer, and need as little typing as possible**, without any JavaScript?

Nobody showed you this in class, beyond `autocomplete` on the name and email. Try your own idea first. If you ask the agent, ask for two or three options and what each costs, not for a fix, and say in `NOTES.md` which you picked and why. Then try it on your own phone, on the live page.

<details>
<summary>Stuck? Look into…</summary>

- input `type`s beyond `text` and `email` (`tel`, `url`, `number`, `date`), and what each does to a phone's keyboard,
- `inputmode`, and when it's better than changing the `type`,
- the full list of `autocomplete` values (`tel`, `postal-code`, `bday`…),
- `enterkeyhint`, and what it changes on the keyboard's Return key.

</details>

In `NOTES.md`, add a section called **Next step: what I tried**: your idea, what happened on your own phone for two fields (which keyboard came up, what filled itself in), and what you'd try next. An idea that didn't work, with a clear account of why, still meets the brief.

## Spec: the "done" list

<!-- done:start -->

**Responsive**

- [ ] Mobile first: the base CSS (outside any media query) is one column, and every media query uses `min-width` in `em`.
- [ ] The layout changes at least once as the window widens (for example, the form and the panel side by side).
- [ ] At 320px wide (DevTools device toolbar), nothing makes the page scroll sideways, and nothing is hidden that a wide screen shows.

**Forms**

- [ ] At least five fields, including an `email` input, a group of radio buttons or checkboxes in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, and a `<textarea>`.
- [ ] Every field has a visible `<label>` joined by `for` and `id`; no `placeholder` does a label's job.
- [ ] Required fields use `required` and say "required" in their label.
- [ ] `autocomplete` on the name and email fields.
- [ ] Problems are styled with `:user-invalid` (not `:invalid`), and shown in words as well as color.
- [ ] The form uses `action="thanks.html" method="get"`, and `thanks.html` is a real page in the same folder, with a link back.
- [ ] A real `<button type="submit">`, at least 44 px tall.

**Next step: add a feature**

- [ ] A real attempt at making the form quick to fill in on a phone (it can still be imperfect).
- [ ] `NOTES.md` has a **Next step: what I tried** section: the idea, what happened on your phone for two fields, and what you'd try next.

**Every Prototype**

- [ ] Landmarks (`header`, `main`, `footer`), and CSS in a separate file shared by both pages.
- [ ] Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast, including field borders against the background (3:1).
- [ ] Passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors, and your **Feedback checks** run shows ✅ for both pages in both tables (or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did).
- [ ] Focus is visible on every field, button, and link.
- [ ] Readable at 200% zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +): nothing cut off, overlapping, or hidden.
- [ ] Linked from your hub under Prototypes. Live page loads.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words. Meaningful commits.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who fills in your form, on what device, and what do they need to know first? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. List your fields and which are required.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: the fields with their types, the base (phone) layout, and the width where the layout changes, with a reason. Build the form first, one column, then add the media query. Leave the next step for last, and try your own idea before asking for help.
4. **Verify:** click every label; submit it empty, then with made-up details; device toolbar at 320; zoom to 200%; Tab through; check contrast, including field borders; open the live page on your own phone and fill it in with one thumb; read the [Feedback checks](/250/kit/#reading-your-checks) Summary after you push.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, including Next step: what I tried, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p04-responsive-forms` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** placeholders instead of labels, radio buttons with no `fieldset`, `:invalid` (every field red before anyone types), errors shown only in red, a JavaScript validation script, `max-width` media queries in `px`, and a pale gray field border under 3:1.
