# P4 · Responsive + Forms

Make a **Suggest a show** page for the Saturday Morning Archive, for visitors who want a cartoon added: a form anyone can fill in, on any screen, that lands them on a thank-you page. It's due Mon Nov 23, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Meets means every "done" item; Exceeds adds one extra. Rework until Dec 9.

It puts Responsive design (Mon Nov 16) and Forms (Wed Nov 18) together, and it's your last Prototype: from Mon Nov 23 your studio time goes to the Final Project, where a form like this one is a good page to reuse. The step most people miss is the error messages: use `:user-invalid`, not `:invalid` (which turns every field red before anyone types), and say what's wrong in words, not only in red.

## The brief

The page has a short intro, a form where visitors suggest a cartoon for the archive, and a panel that says what happens to suggestions. When someone submits, they land on a thank-you page in the same folder. GitHub Pages can't store the answers, and that's fine: the work is in a form anyone can fill in, on any screen.

On a phone, it's one column: intro, form, panel. When there's room, the form and the panel sit side by side.

Own-choice option: a form for any real or realistic audience (a club sign-up, an event RSVP, a feedback form). Say who in your Intent.

## Spec: the "done" list

<!-- done:start -->

**Responsive**

- [ ] Mobile first: the base CSS (outside any media query) is one column, and every media query uses `min-width` in `em`.
- [ ] The layout changes at least once as the window widens (for example, the form and the panel side by side).
- [ ] At 320px wide (DevTools device toolbar), nothing makes the page scroll sideways, and nothing is hidden that a wide screen shows.

**Forms**

- [ ] At least five fields, including an `email` input, a group of radio buttons or checkboxes in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, and a `<textarea>`.
- [ ] Every field has a visible `<label>` joined by `for` and `id`; no `placeholder` does a label's job.
- [ ] Required fields use `required` and say "required" in their label.
- [ ] `autocomplete` on the name and email fields.
- [ ] Problems are styled with `:user-invalid` (not `:invalid`), and shown in words as well as color.
- [ ] The form uses `action="thanks.html" method="get"`, and `thanks.html` is a real page in the same folder, with a link back.
- [ ] A real `<button type="submit">`, at least 44 px tall.

**Every Prototype**

- [ ] Landmarks (`header`, `main`, `footer`), and CSS in a separate file shared by both pages.
- [ ] Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast, including field borders against the background (3:1).
- [ ] Passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors, and your **Feedback checks** run shows ✅ for both pages in both tables (or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did).
- [ ] Focus is visible on every field, button, and link.
- [ ] Readable at 200% zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +): nothing cut off, overlapping, or hidden.
- [ ] Linked from your hub under Prototypes. Live page loads.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words. Meaningful commits.

<!-- done:end -->

**Stretch goal (one way to reach Exceeds):** give every field that has a format or a limit a hint tied to it with `aria-describedby`, then test the whole form with VoiceOver (Cmd + F5) or NVDA: Tab through it, submit it empty, and write in `NOTES.md` what the screen reader said for two fields and the radio group.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who suggests a show, on what device, and what do they need to know first? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. List your fields and which are required.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: the fields with their types, the base (phone) layout, and the width where the layout changes, with a reason. Build the form first, one column, then add the media query.
4. **Verify:** click every label; submit it empty, then with made-up details; device toolbar at 320; zoom to 200%; Tab through; check contrast, including field borders; read the Feedback checks Summary after you push.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p04-responsive-forms` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** placeholders instead of labels, radio buttons with no `fieldset`, `:invalid` (every field red before anyone types), errors shown only in red, a JavaScript validation script, `max-width` media queries in `px`, and a pale gray field border under 3:1.
