# P1 · Color + Typography

Build a small three-page site: two pages in very different themes, such as an academic article and a fan site, each with its own colors and type, and a homepage that explains why each choice fits its page. It's due Mon Nov 2, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Meets means every "done" item; Exceeds adds one extra. Rework until Dec 9.

It puts the Color unit (Mon Oct 26) and the Typography unit (Wed Oct 28) together, and it's all new pages: S03 and S04 taught you how, but don't copy their files. Make the two themes far apart, so the choices have to differ. A Saturday Morning Archive fan page is a good choice for one of them. Check every color pair on both pages in WebAIM, not just the ratios the agent states: agents often get them wrong. Then run the W3C validator and zoom to 200% on all three pages before you call it done.

## The brief

Someone lands on your homepage. It shows them two pages that each feel right for their audience at a glance, and tells them how you got there: which colors and fonts each page uses, why they fit, and proof that every page stays readable for everyone, including people with low vision or color blindness, and anyone reading on a phone in sunlight.

Two themes that work well together: a serious one (an academic article, a museum label, a news story) and a playful one (a fan site, a party invitation, a cereal box). Own-choice option: any two themes with a real or realistic audience; say who in your Intent.

## Spec: the "done" list

**The site**

- [ ] `prototypes/p01-color-typography/` has three pages: `index.html` (the homepage) and two themed pages, all new, not copies of an earlier Spark.
- [ ] A nav on every page links all three pages.
- [ ] The homepage explains each themed page's palette and fonts, with a reason from the theme for each choice, and shows each page's contrast results.

**Color, on each themed page**

- [ ] Its own palette of 3–5 colors in one format (hex, `rgb()`, or `hsl()`), used consistently.
- [ ] At least one background beyond plain white: a color, a gradient, or an image with a fallback color.
- [ ] Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text), checked with [WebAIM's contrast checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/). The results are on the homepage.
- [ ] Color never carries meaning alone: links are distinguishable without color (underline, or similar).

**Typography, on each themed page**

- [ ] One or two web fonts of its own, each with a fallback stack ending in a generic family (`serif`, `sans-serif`), loading only the weights it uses.
- [ ] Font sizes in `rem` (or `em` where it's relative to the parent on purpose). No `px` font sizes.
- [ ] Body `line-height` of at least 1.5, and line length kept readable (about 45–75 characters).
- [ ] A heading hierarchy that's clear from the type alone, in a logical order (`h1` → `h2` → `h3`).

**Every Prototype (all three pages)**

- [ ] Landmarks (`header`, `main`, `footer`), and CSS in separate files.
- [ ] Passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors.
- [ ] Focus is visible on every link.
- [ ] Readable at 200% zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +): nothing cut off, overlapping, or hidden.
- [ ] Linked from your hub under Prototypes. Live pages load.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words. Meaningful commits.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** name your two themes, who each page is for, and what each should feel like. Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. Add each theme's mood or era if it helps the agent.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: the three pages, then for each themed page a palette with contrast ratios and two or three font pairings. Build one themed page at a time, then the homepage. Check the ratios yourself, and write the homepage's reasons from your own choices.
4. **Verify:** check contrast for every pair on both themed pages, run the validator on all three, Tab through each page, and zoom to 200% (Cmd/Ctrl +): is everything still readable with nothing cut off?
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p01-color-typography` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** two themes that end up looking alike, light-gray text on white, links shown only by color, `px` font sizes, `outline: none`, a font loaded without a fallback, and homepage reasons that would fit any page ("clean and modern").
