# P2 · Box model + Flexbox

**Units:** Box model and display (Mon Nov 2) and Flexbox (Wed Nov 4). No late penalty; rework is welcome until Dec 9.

## The brief

Make a **Shows** page for the Saturday Morning Archive: a header with the site's title and a nav, then a card for each of four to six shows, then a footer. Each card has the show's name as a heading, one or two sentences about it, and a link (to a page of yours, or to the show's page on a site you trust). The content is yours, and it can be short. The work is in the **layout**: boxes sized on purpose, cards that wrap neatly at any width, and links big enough to tap.

Bring your P1 look with you if you like: your palette and type are a good start, and contrast still has to pass.

Own-choice option: a page of cards on any topic with a real or realistic audience (a club's events, a team roster, a reading list). Say who in your Intent.

## Spec: the "done" list

<!-- done:start -->

**Box model**

- [ ] `box-sizing: border-box` on every element, at the top of the CSS.
- [ ] Each card has padding and a border or background, and the space between cards comes from `gap`, not margins on each card.
- [ ] Spacing uses two or three sizes in `rem`, reused throughout (list them in `NOTES.md`).
- [ ] Every link is at least 24 × 24 px (inline links in a sentence are exempt), and the nav links are at least 44 px tall. Check them in DevTools.

**Flexbox**

- [ ] The header is a flex row (title and nav) that wraps when there isn't room.
- [ ] The cards sit in a flex container with `flex-wrap: wrap` and `gap`.
- [ ] Each card is a flex column, so its link lines up at the bottom of every card in a row (hint: `margin-top: auto` on the link).
- [ ] The order you see is the order in the HTML: no `order`, no `-reverse` direction, and Tab follows what you see.
- [ ] Each card's link text is different, and says where it goes.

**Every Prototype**

- [ ] Landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `footer`), and CSS in a separate file.
- [ ] Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text).
- [ ] Passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors, and your **Feedback checks** run shows ✅ for this page in both tables (or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did).
- [ ] Focus is visible on every link.
- [ ] Readable at 200% zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +): nothing cut off, overlapping, or hidden.
- [ ] Linked from your hub under Prototypes. Live page loads.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words. Meaningful commits.

<!-- done:end -->

**Stretch goal (one way to reach Exceeds):** the cards go from one column on a narrow window to three on a wide one using only `flex` (for example `flex: 1 1 15rem`) and `flex-wrap`, with no media queries. In `NOTES.md`, explain how the browser decides when a card drops to the next line.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who is this page for, and what should they be able to do on it? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. Add the number of cards and what's on each.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: which elements are flex containers, in which direction, and how tall the nav links will be. Build it in small steps (header, then cards, then the inside of a card).
4. **Verify:** hover each link in DevTools for its size, Tab through the whole page, zoom to 200%, check new color pairs, and read the Feedback checks Summary after you push.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p02-box-model-flexbox` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** text-sized nav links with no padding, `width: 100%` plus padding with no `border-box`, cards with fixed `px` widths or heights, `order` or `row-reverse` to rearrange cards, and "Read more" on every card.
