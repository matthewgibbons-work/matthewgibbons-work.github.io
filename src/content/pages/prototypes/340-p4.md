# P4 · Team planner

## In short

Build a Svelte team planner for a trainer: add up to six Pokémon by name, give each a nickname and a role in a real form, and see a summary that stays up to date, from typed components that share one team. Then add a feature that shows the team's weak spots, and write up what you tried in `NOTES.md`.

## The problem

Three friends are entering a tournament and planning one team together. Each of them suggests Pokémon, gives them nicknames, and argues about who attacks and who defends. Today it's a group chat: nicknames get lost, two people add the same Pokémon, and nobody can see at a glance how many attackers the team has. Worst of all, nobody notices until the first battle that half the team goes down to the same type of attack.

S08 and S09 solved the pieces: components that pass data down and events up, and forms that everyone can use. P4 puts them together into a planner: one shared team that every part of the page sees, a real form for each member, and a summary that's always right. The next step adds a feature the group chat never had: **show the team's weak spots before the first battle.**

**Like P3, it has a required next step** (below) that adds a feature beyond the first problem. Trying it and writing up what happened is part of Meets; getting it working is a way to reach Exceeds.

It's due Tue Nov 24, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Rework until Dec 9.

It builds on Svelte components and props (Mon Nov 16) and Svelte forms and shared state (Wed Nov 18), and it's a good first piece of your [Final Project](/340/final-project/): next week it can move into your Astro site as an island. Under the Learning style, write any `TODO(human)` yourself (a validation rule, or which component owns focus). The step most people miss is the form's errors: a visible `<label>`, and an error in words that's announced, not just a red border. Own-choice option: another free, no-key JSON API where planning a small group makes sense, by proposal to the instructor first.

## The brief

Each member gets a role (attacker, defender, or support), and the summary shows how many of each role and which types the team has. It's P3's party builder grown up: the page is split into components with typed props, the team lives in one shared module that every component reads, and editing a member is a real form, with labels and errors that everyone can perceive.

## Next step: add a feature

The friends want to know before the tournament: **which types of attack would hit two or more of their members hard, and how can the page tell them in words, not just colours?**

Nobody showed you this in class. The types each member has are already in the PokéAPI data you fetch, but what each type is weak to isn't. Try your own idea first. If you ask Claude, ask for two or three options and what each costs (how many requests, what happens when one fails), not for a fix, and say in `NOTES.md` which you picked and why.

<details>
<summary>Stuck? Look into…</summary>

- PokéAPI's `/type/{name}` endpoint, and its `damage_relations`,
- `Promise.all`, and how many requests a six-member team really needs,
- testing a function with a small, made-up list of types, so Vitest never calls the API.

</details>

In `NOTES.md`, add a section called **Next step: what I tried**: your idea, what it showed for your own team (and for a team you built to be weak on purpose), and what you'd try next. An idea that didn't work, with a clear account of why, still meets the brief.

## Spec: the "done" list

**Components and props**

- [ ] `prototypes/p04-team-planner/` is a Svelte 5 + Vite project in JavaScript (TypeScript only for typed props), with `base: './'` and no template leftovers.
- [ ] At least four components, for example `TeamList`, `TeamMember`, `MemberForm`, and `TeamSummary`. Each component's props are typed with an `interface Props`.
- [ ] Children tell their parent what happened through callback props. No component changes data it was only handed (no `ownership_invalid_mutation` warnings in the browser console).
- [ ] One layout component renders `children` in a `<section>` labelled by its heading.

**Shared state**

- [ ] The team lives in a `.svelte.js` module (an exported object whose properties change), imported by at least two components. Nothing copies it into a second `$state` with an `$effect`.
- [ ] The team's rules (at most six, no duplicates, a nickname of 1–12 characters) are plain functions with Vitest tests.

**The form**

- [ ] Editing a member uses a form with `bind:value` for the nickname and `bind:group` (radio buttons in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`) for the role. Every control has a visible `<label>`.
- [ ] On an invalid submit: an error in words next to the field, `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby` on the input, the error announced, and focus on the first field to fix.
- [ ] Adding by name fetches from PokéAPI with a `response.ok` check, loading and error states, and an announced result.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who plans teams, and what do they need to see while they do it? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above.
3. **Generate:** plan mode first, and ask for the plan as a list of components with each one's props and callbacks, and which ones import the shared module. Then: the rules module and tests, the shared module, the list and members with hard-coded data, the summary, the form, and the fetch. Leave the next step for last, and try your own idea before asking for help.
4. **Verify:** submit the form empty, too long, and with only spaces, keyboard only, with VoiceOver on (Cmd+F5); rename someone and watch the summary; add seven and a duplicate; keep both consoles open; build a team that's weak on purpose and check the weak spots say so; zoom to 200%; check both runs in **Actions**.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, including Next step: what I tried, then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `prototypes/p04-team-planner` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** placeholders instead of labels, errors shown only in red, a child mutating its props, `$effect`s copying state between components, an exported `let` that Svelte refuses to build, and Svelte 4 syntax (`export let`, `createEventDispatcher`, `on:submit`).
