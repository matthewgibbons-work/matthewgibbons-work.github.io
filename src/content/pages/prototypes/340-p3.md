# P3 · Party builder

## In short

Build a Svelte party builder for a trainer: look Pokémon up by name, keep the party legal (six at most, no duplicates) with rules you've tested, and filter it by type, with zero accessibility warnings. Then try to make the party survive closing the tab, and write up what you tried in `NOTES.md`.

## The problem

A trainer is getting ready for a gym battle against water types. They look up candidates one at a time, try them in a party, swap some out, and want to see "only my electric types" before deciding. Today they do it in a notes app: they lose track of who's already in, end up with seven, and can't filter. And when they close the tab and come back tomorrow, the party is gone.

S07 solved the first part: a party shown with Svelte's blocks that says what's happening and works with the keyboard. P3 makes it a builder: look up by name, keep the party legal (six at most, no duplicates), and filter it by type, with no accessibility warnings from the compiler. The beyond is the part S07 didn't touch: **keep the party when the tab closes.**

**New in this Prototype:** a required "beyond" part (below). Trying it and writing up what happened is part of Meets; getting it working is a way to reach Exceeds.

It's due Tue Nov 17, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Rework until Dec 9.

It's one unit, Svelte logic and events (Mon Nov 9); there's no class Wed Nov 11, so the week has a longer studio. Under the Learning style, write any `TODO(human)` yourself (the "party full" message or the filter's `$derived` are likely). Keep the two rules (six at most, no duplicates) in a plain module with Vitest tests, as in P2. The step most people miss is the filter: make it a `$derived` from the party, not a second copy in `$state` that drifts. Own-choice option: another free, no-key JSON API where building a small collection makes sense, by proposal to the instructor first.

## The brief

Everything you've done since S02 comes together here, but now Svelte's blocks carry the logic: `{#each}` with a key for the party, `{#if}` for empty and full, and `{#await}` (or one clear status variable) for each lookup. "Show me only my water types" is the filter a trainer wants.

## The beyond (required)

The trainer builds a party, closes the tab, and comes back tomorrow. **How can the party still be there, and what should happen if what's stored isn't what your app expects** (an older version's data, a hand-edited value, or nothing at all)?

Nobody showed you this in class, and S07's Watch for warned you off one of the tools it needs (for fetching, which is a different job). Try your own idea first. If you ask Claude, ask for two or three options and what each costs, not for a fix, and say in `NOTES.md` which you picked and why.

<details>
<summary>Stuck? Look into…</summary>

- `localStorage`, and what it can and can't store,
- `$effect`, and why saving somewhere outside the component is the job it's for (when fetching wasn't),
- `JSON.parse`, and what it does with text it doesn't expect.

</details>

In `NOTES.md`, add a section called **Beyond: what I tried**: your idea, what happened when you reloaded (and when you broke the stored data on purpose, in DevTools → **Application** → **Local storage**), and what you'd try next. An idea that didn't work, with a clear account of why, still meets the brief.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who builds parties, what do they need to see while they do, and what should be waiting for them when they come back? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above.
3. **Generate:** plan mode first, then small steps: the project, the rules module and its tests (break one on purpose), a hard-coded party with remove and filter, then the lookup form. Leave the beyond for last, and try your own idea before asking for help.
4. **Verify:** keep the `npm run dev` terminal visible for warnings; add seven, add a duplicate, misspell one, filter to a type, then remove everyone of that type (does the "no one matches" message show?), remove everyone with the keyboard; throttle to Slow 4G and double-press Add; reload with a party built, then break the stored data in DevTools and reload again; zoom to 200%; check both runs in **Actions**.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, including Beyond: what I tried, then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `prototypes/p03-party-builder` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** an unkeyed `{#each}`, a filter that copies the party into a second `$state` (and drifts from it), clickable `<div>`s for type chips, a type shown only as a colour, and rules written inside the component where the tests can't reach them.
