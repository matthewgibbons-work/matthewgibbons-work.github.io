# P1 · Async party

Build a party page from live [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co/) data for someone planning a team: up to six Pokémon with names, sprites, and types, plus a form to add one more by name, with loading and error messages that everyone, including screen-reader users, can perceive. It's due Mon Nov 2, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Meets means every "done" item; Exceeds adds one extra. Rework until Dec 9.

It builds on Async 1 (`Promise.all`) and Async 2 (`fetch`, `response.ok`, errors). The form and the screen-reader announcements are the two new pieces: the Async 2 unit page's "From a form to the page" shows their shape, a `submit` listener with `preventDefault()` and an `aria-live` paragraph that's on the page from the start. Under the Learning style, write any `TODO(human)` yourself (error handling is a likely one). The step most people miss is fetching the starting party in parallel: one `Promise.all`, not an `await` inside a loop. Own-choice option: another free, no-key JSON API, by proposal to the instructor first.

## The brief

A user can see a party of up to six Pokémon (name, sprite, and types) fetched live, and can add one more by name. The network is slow and people make typos, so the page has to handle waiting and failure well, and tell *everyone* what's happening.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who uses this page, and what do they need from it? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above.
3. **Generate:** plan mode first, then small steps: fetch one Pokémon, then the party, then the form, then the states.
4. **Verify:** open the console, throttle the network in DevTools (Network → Slow 4G) to see your loading state, try a misspelled name, test with the keyboard, and zoom to 200%. Turn on VoiceOver or NVDA briefly to hear the announcements.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `prototypes/p01-async-party` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** sequential `await`s in a loop where `Promise.all` was asked for, missing `response.ok` checks, `.then()` chains mixed with `async`/`await`, errors only logged to the console, and sprites with no `alt`.
