# P3 · Grid

**Units:** Grid (Mon Nov 9). No late penalty; rework is welcome until Dec 9.

## The brief

Make a **home page** for the Saturday Morning Archive: a header with the site's title and a nav, a short welcome, a gallery of at least six show tiles, a sidebar (for example "This week's pick" or "New in the archive"), and a footer. Each tile has the show's name as a heading, a line or two about it, and a link. Images are optional; if you use any, they need useful `alt` text and a `width` and `height`.

The work is in the **layout**: a page drawn with named areas, and a gallery that decides its own number of columns. P3 has one unit and one class this week, so keep the words short and spend your time on the grid.

The page needs to work on a laptop and at 200% zoom. The phone layout for the whole page is next week's unit (and P4), but the gallery should already fit any width.

Own-choice option: a home page for any site with a real or realistic audience. Say who in your Intent.

## Spec: the "done" list

<!-- done:start -->

**Grid**

- [ ] The page layout uses `grid-template-areas` with at least `header`, `main`, `aside`, and `footer`, and each element is placed with `grid-area`.
- [ ] The gallery uses `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(…, 1fr))` and `gap`, with no fixed number of columns.
- [ ] As the window narrows, the gallery drops to fewer columns, down to one, without making the page scroll sideways.
- [ ] Tiles have no fixed `height`: at 200% zoom, no text spills out of its tile.
- [ ] Each tile has a heading and a link whose text says where it goes.
- [ ] The order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.

**Every Prototype**

- [ ] Landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `aside`, `footer`), and CSS in a separate file.
- [ ] Every text/background pair meets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text).
- [ ] Passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors, and your **Feedback checks** run shows ✅ for this page in both tables (or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did).
- [ ] Focus is visible on every link, and every link is at least 24 × 24 px (links in a sentence are exempt).
- [ ] Readable at 200% zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +): nothing cut off, overlapping, or hidden.
- [ ] Linked from your hub under Prototypes. Live page loads.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words. Meaningful commits.

<!-- done:end -->

**Stretch goal (one way to reach Exceeds):** make each tile span three rows of the gallery with `grid-template-rows: subgrid`, so the headings, text, and links line up across a row even when one tile has more text than the others. In `NOTES.md`, explain what the tile takes from its parent grid.

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who arrives on this home page, and what should they find first? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. Add the shows you'll feature and what goes in the sidebar.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: the area strings, and the gallery's `grid-template-columns`. Read the strings before you agree. Build the page layout first, then the gallery, then the inside of a tile.
4. **Verify:** turn on DevTools' grid badge and area names, drag the window narrower, Tab through, zoom to 200%, check new color pairs, and read the Feedback checks Summary after you push.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p03-grid` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** a fixed `repeat(3, 1fr)`, a "featured" tile with `grid-column: span 2` (it forces sideways scrolling when only one column fits), tiles with a fixed `height`, areas drawn in a different order from the HTML, and "Read more" on every tile.
