# P3 · Grid

## The problem

Someone lands on a site's home page for the first time. In a few seconds they need to know **what the site is**, **browse what it has** on whatever screen they're holding, and **spot the one thing worth starting with**. Most first drafts manage the first two and fail the third: every tile looks the same, or the "featured" tile is made bigger in a way that breaks the page on a narrow window.

Your job is a home page that does all three. S07 solved part of it: a page drawn with named areas, and cards that fit any width. P3 adds the part S07 didn't: a featured item that stands out at every width.

**New in this Prototype:** a required "beyond" part (below). Trying it and writing up what happened is part of Meets; getting it working is a way to reach Exceeds.

It's due Tue Nov 17, with no late penalty. Submit by pasting two links in Learning Suite. Rework until Dec 9. It has one unit, Grid (Mon Nov 9), and there's no class Wed Nov 11, so keep the words short and spend your time on the grid. The page needs to work on a laptop and at 200% zoom; the phone layout for the whole page comes with Responsive design (Mon Nov 16) and P4, but the gallery and the featured item have to fit any width already.

## The brief

A home page for a first-time visitor: a header with the site's title and a nav, a short welcome, a gallery of at least six tiles, one of them **featured**, a sidebar for a returning visitor (what's new, or opening hours), and a footer. Each tile has a heading, a line or two, and a link. Images are optional; if you use any, they need useful `alt` text and a `width` and `height`. Pick a collection:

- the **Saturday Morning Archive**: shows, with this week's pick featured,
- a farmers market: vendors, with the vendor of the week,
- a trail guide: hikes near campus, with the trail of the month,
- a campus club fair: clubs, with the one that's new this year,
- a recipe collection: recipes, with the dish of the week,
- or your own idea, for a real or realistic audience. Say who in your Intent. An S06 or S07 topic is fine, with a new page and new words.

The work is in the **layout**: a page drawn with named areas, a gallery that decides its own number of columns, and a featured tile that stands out.

## The beyond (required)

Your visitor should spot the featured tile at a glance, so it should stand out from the rest, and bigger is the obvious way. But the obvious fix, `grid-column: span 2`, breaks the page when only one column fits (you saw it in the Live demo). **How can one tile stand out at every width, down to one column, without the page scrolling sideways, and without Tab jumping around?**

There's more than one good answer, and nobody showed you this one in class. Try your own idea first. If you ask the agent, ask for two or three options and what each costs, not for a fix, and say in `NOTES.md` which you picked and why.

<details>
<summary>Stuck? Look into…</summary>

- grid line numbers in `grid-column`, and what `-1` means,
- `grid-auto-flow: dense`, and what it does to the order Tab follows,
- `min()` inside `minmax()`.

</details>

In `NOTES.md`, add a section called **Beyond: what I tried**: your idea, what happened when you dragged the window down to one column, and what you'd try next. An idea that didn't work, with a clear account of why, still meets the brief.

## Spec: the "done" list

<!-- done:start -->

**Grid**

- [ ] The page layout uses `grid-template-areas` with at least `header`, `main`, `aside`, and `footer`, and each element is placed with `grid-area`.
- [ ] The gallery uses `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(…, 1fr))` and `gap`, with no fixed number of columns.
- [ ] As the window narrows, the gallery drops to fewer columns, down to one, without making the page scroll sideways.
- [ ] Tiles have no fixed `height`: at 200% zoom, no text spills out of its tile.
- [ ] Each tile has a heading and a link whose text says where it goes.
- [ ] The order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.

**The beyond**

- [ ] One tile is marked as featured and styled to stand out (it can still be imperfect at some widths).

## Working in the loop

1. **Intent:** who arrives on this home page, what should they find first, and why is your featured tile the place to start? Write it in `NOTES.md` first.
2. **Spec:** start from the list above. Add the tiles you'll include and what goes in the sidebar.
3. **Generate:** ask for a plan first: the area strings, and the gallery's `grid-template-columns`. Read the strings before you agree. Build the page layout first, then the gallery, then the inside of a tile. Leave the featured tile for last, and try your own idea before asking for help.
4. **Verify:** turn on DevTools' grid badge and area names, drag the window down to one column (watch the featured tile), Tab through, zoom to 200%, check new color pairs, and read the Feedback checks Summary after you push.
5. **Explain:** finish `NOTES.md`, including Beyond: what I tried, then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `prototypes/p03-grid` and paste in the "done" list (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Watch for:** a fixed `repeat(3, 1fr)`, tiles with a fixed `height`, areas drawn in a different order from the HTML, a featured tile moved to the front with CSS while it's later in the HTML, and "Read more" on every tile.
