# S10 · Clip of the week

## The problem

You're putting up a "Clip of the week" page: one short clip at the top, and cards for the clips from earlier weeks below it. The first draft works for someone who can see and hear everything, and fails nearly everyone else. The clip **starts on its own, with sound**, and has no buttons to stop it. It has **no captions**. Every **"New" badge lands in the page's top corner**, stacked on top of each other, instead of on its own card. And each card's ▶ link has no words, so a screen reader announces only **"link"**.

Your job is the "Wanted" side: a new clip page anyone can watch, hear about, and navigate. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Nov 30 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

It's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark. Use the unit's [At a glance](/250/units/multimedia/#at-a-glance) table and Live demo as your reference. Writing the captions is the part only you can do: the agent can't hear the clip, so listen and write every line yourself. The step most people miss is `position: relative` on the card: without it, every badge lands in one corner of the page.

**Your Final Project's next milestone is due Mon Nov 30 too:** your site's shell, live. It's ungraded, and it's on the [Final Project](/250/final-project/#milestones) brief's milestone map.

## 1. Intent

Create the new folder `sparks/s10-multimedia/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to). Download MDN's free sample clip, [friday.mp4](https://developer.mozilla.org/shared-assets/videos/friday.mp4) (six seconds from the 1940 film *His Girl Friday*), into it. Then write two or three sentences in its **Intent** section: whose page it is, who watches, and who needs the captions, and when ("someone on the bus with no headphones" is one; "someone who is Deaf" is another). Pick a page:

- a film club's clip of the week, with cards for the last few meetings,
- a language class's listening practice, with cards for earlier clips,
- a **Saturday Morning Archive** watch party teaser, with cards for past parties,
- a "classic movie lines" page, with cards for other famous lines,
- or your own idea, for a real or realistic audience.

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Look at the sketch and the unit's At a glance table, and name the elements, attributes, and properties (not their values) you expect to fix each part of the problem: the clip that plays on its own, the missing captions, the badges in the page's corner, and the link with no words. A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and ask it to propose a plan before it writes any HTML. Then have it build the page.

```text
Here's my intent and my "done" list for sparks/s10-multimedia/. Before you write any code, list the page's parts in order, which element each one is, and which element each badge is positioned against. Don't write friday.vtt: I'll write the captions myself. Wait for me to agree.
```

Write `friday.vtt` yourself: play the clip, pause after each line, and type what you hear with its start and end time. You can ask the agent what the WebVTT format looks like, but if it offers to write the captions, say no: it would be guessing.

**Watch for:** `autoplay`, `muted`, or `loop` on the video; no `controls`; captions the agent made up; badges that pile up in one corner of the page; "New" shown only as a color; and a link name hidden with `display: none`. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch on your own page:

- Reload the page: nothing plays until you press play. Turn on captions with the CC button and watch the whole clip. Do your captions match the words and the timing?
- Tab to the video controls and play it with the keyboard.
- Is each "New" badge on its own card? Zoom to 200%: does it cover any text or link?
- Turn on VoiceOver (Cmd + F5) or NVDA: does it say "New" on the badged card, and the name of your icon link?
- Read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push.

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: the elements, attributes, and properties the agent actually used for each part of the problem, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why. A wrong guess you can now explain is worth more than a right one you can't.

Then finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `position: absolute` or the `.visually-hidden` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s10-multimedia` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
