# S10 · Media and badges

Add media to your S08 page: an image with a caption, a short video with captions you write yourself, a "New" badge in a card's corner, and an icon link that screen readers can name. Plan on 15–30 minutes, then go on to your Final Project. It's due before class on Mon Nov 30 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

Writing the captions is the part only you can do: the agent can't hear the clip, so listen and write every line yourself. Keep `controls` and leave out `autoplay`. The step most people miss is `position: relative` on the card: without it, every badge lands in one corner of the page.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s08-responsive/` folder to `sparks/s10-multimedia/` (leave S08 as it is). Download MDN's free sample clip, [friday.mp4](https://developer.mozilla.org/shared-assets/videos/friday.mp4), into the new folder. Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: who needs the captions, and when. "Someone watching on the bus with no headphones" is one; "someone who is Deaf" is another.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first. Write `friday.vtt` yourself: play the clip, pause after each line, and type what you hear with its start and end time. You can ask the agent what the WebVTT format looks like, but if it offers to write the captions, say no: it would be guessing.

**Watch for:** `autoplay`, `muted`, or `loop` on the video; no `controls`; captions the agent made up; badges that pile up in one corner of the page; "New" shown only as a color; and a label hidden with `display: none`. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Reload the page: nothing plays until you press play. Turn on captions with the CC button and watch the whole clip. Do your captions match the words and the timing?
- Tab to the video controls and play it with the keyboard.
- Turn on VoiceOver (Cmd + F5) or NVDA: does it say "New" on the badged card, and the name of your icon link?
- Zoom to 200%, then read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `position: absolute` or the `.visually-hidden` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s10-multimedia` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
