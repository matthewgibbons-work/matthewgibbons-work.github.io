# S09 · Release intent brief

**Concept:** Intent and Spec, the two steps of the loop the agent can't do for you, at the size of a final project.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

Before anything gets generated, decide what your [Release](/340/release/) is. This Spark is a short document, written by you: who it's for, what problem it solves, where the data comes from, and what "done" will look like. Then Claude plays the client who has to sign off on it: it asks hard questions, and you decide what to change. Claude doesn't write any of it. If you're proposing your own API, this is your proposal.

## 1. Intent

Your `NOTES.md` **Intent** for this Spark is short: why you picked this project over the others you considered.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s09-release-intent/INTENT.md` exists, written by you, with these sections:
  - **Audience:** who uses the site, specifically (not "Pokémon fans").
  - **Problem:** what they can't do easily now, in two or three sentences.
  - **Data:** the API, and the two or three endpoints you'll use. You've opened each one in the browser and named the fields you need.
  - **Pages and islands:** each page, one line each, marked "built" (data at build time) or "island" (interactive in the browser), with the `client:` directive you expect to use.
  - **My "done" list:** 6–10 checkable items for your Release, beyond the course's list.
  - **Risks:** the one or two things most likely to go wrong, and your plan B.
- [ ] You ran the review prompt below, and `INTENT.md` has a short **Changes after review** section: what you changed and what you kept, and why.
- [ ] If you're using your own API: it's free, needs no key, and returns JSON; the Data section says so.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to the folder on GitHub under Sparks (there's no page).
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed and pushed.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate (the review, not the writing)

Write `INTENT.md` yourself, then paste this into your `dight340` session:

```text
Read sparks/s09-release-intent/INTENT.md and the Release brief's "done" list. Don't edit any file. Act as the client who has to approve this project: ask me five hard questions about it, one at a time, and wait for my answer to each. Then tell me which items in my "done" list couldn't be checked by someone else, and which page is most likely to be too big for the time left.
```

Answer the questions in the chat, then change `INTENT.md` yourself.

**Watch for:** Claude offering to "tidy up" or rewrite the brief (say no: it's your intent), "done" items like "looks good" or "is user-friendly" that nobody can check, and a plan with more islands than pages.

## 4. Verify

- Open every endpoint in your Data section in the browser. Does it return what you named?
- Read your "done" list as if it were someone else's. Could you check each item in under a minute?
- Count the pages and islands against the timeline in the Release brief. Is there a week for each?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`. In **AI use**, put the review prompt and the one question that changed your plan. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on Astro's `client:` directives (you'll meet them on Dec 2) is a good dig-deeper starting point.

Then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `sparks/s09-release-intent` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
