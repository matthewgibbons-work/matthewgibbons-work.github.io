# S09 · Final Project intent brief

Write a short intent brief for your [Final Project](/340/final-project/), yourself: who your Digital Humanities site is for, a first draft of your definition, your examples, where the live data comes from, its pages and islands, and what "done" will look like. Then have Claude question it like a client, and decide what to change. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Nov 23 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

Claude doesn't write any of it: say no if it offers to tidy it up or define DH for you. It can help you *find* DH projects to look at, but you choose them and open each one yourself. If you're proposing an API that isn't on the brief's list, this is your proposal. Your instructor reads every intent brief before Monday and replies on anything that won't fit, so push it on time. The step most people miss is a "done" list someone else could check: "looks good" can't be checked; "every example page shows its object's museum image and credit" can.

## 1. Intent

Your `NOTES.md` **Intent** for this Spark is short: why you picked these examples over the others you looked at.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s09-final-project-intent/INTENT.md` exists, written by you, with these sections:
  - **Reader:** one real or realistic person the site is for, and the question they'd ask you (not "people curious about DH").
  - **My definition, first draft:** two to four sentences, in your own words. It can change by Dec 16; your reflections page will say how.
  - **Examples:** three or more real DH projects, each linked, with one sentence on why it fits your definition. You've opened each link.
  - **Data:** the API (Art Institute of Chicago, the Met, Wikipedia, or your own proposal), and the two or three endpoints you'll use. You've opened each one in the browser and named the fields you need, and you've said which example each one serves.
  - **Pages and islands:** each page, one line each, marked "built" (data at build time) or "island" (interactive in the browser), with the `client:` directive you expect to use.
  - **My "done" list:** 6–10 checkable items for your Final Project, beyond the course's list.
  - **Risks:** the one or two things most likely to go wrong, and your plan B.
- [ ] You ran the review prompt below, and `INTENT.md` has a short **Changes after review** section: what you changed and what you kept, and why.
- [ ] If you're proposing an API that isn't on the brief's list: it's free, needs no key, and returns JSON; the Data section says so.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to the folder on GitHub under Sparks (there's no page).
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed and pushed.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate (the review, not the writing)

Write `INTENT.md` yourself, then paste this into your `dight340` session, with the [Final Project](/340/final-project/)'s "done" list pasted after it (its **Copy** button copies it; Claude can't see the course site):

```text
Read sparks/s09-final-project-intent/INTENT.md and the Final Project brief's "done" list, pasted below. Don't edit any file. Act as the reader my brief names: ask me five hard questions about my definition, my examples, and my plan, one at a time, and wait for my answer to each. Don't suggest a definition or examples of your own. Then tell me which items in my "done" list couldn't be checked by someone else, where the API data doesn't seem to help that reader, and which page is most likely to be too big for the time left.
```

Answer the questions in the chat, then change `INTENT.md` yourself.

**Watch for:** Claude offering to "tidy up" or rewrite the brief, or to draft your definition (say no: it's your intent), projects or links it made up, "done" items like "looks good" or "is user-friendly" that nobody can check, and a plan with more islands than pages.

## 4. Verify

- Open every endpoint in your Data section in the browser. Does it return what you named? (The Met's search is `/v1.1/search`; the old `/v1/search` is retired.)
- Open each example's link. Does it go to the project itself, and is it still online?
- Read your "done" list as if it were someone else's. Could you check each item in under a minute?
- Count the pages and islands against the timeline in the Final Project brief. Is there a week for each?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`. In **AI use**, put the review prompt and the one question that changed your plan. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on Astro's `client:` directives (you'll meet them on Dec 2) is a good dig-deeper starting point.

Then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `sparks/s09-final-project-intent` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
