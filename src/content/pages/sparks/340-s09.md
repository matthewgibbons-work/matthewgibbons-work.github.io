# S09 · Party nicknames

## The problem

Your S08 party page is in components now. Your friend wants to **nickname** each member, and to see a **Team summary** beside the party ("3 members: Sparky, eevee, snorlax"). Their first try works for them and almost no one else: the box's only label is placeholder text that **vanishes as you type**; pressing **Enter reloads the page** and loses everything; a nickname that's too long gets a **red border and nothing else**, so a screen reader says nothing and nobody knows what's wrong; after an error the cursor is somewhere else; and the summary keeps **its own copy of the party**, so it still says "pikachu" after you've saved "Sparky".

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s09-party-nicknames/` starts as a copy of your S08 project (Svelte 5 + Vite, `base: './'`), and everything S08 did still works.
- [ ] The party lives in `src/party.svelte.js`, exported as a `const` `$state`, with exported functions that change it (rename, remove, level up). No component keeps its own copy, and the build has no `state_invalid_export` error.
- [ ] A nickname form, in its own component, has a visible `<label>` joined by `for` and `id`, with no `placeholder` standing in for it. It lets you pick which member to rename (radio buttons in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` and `bind:group`, or a labeled `<select>`), and the nickname box uses `bind:value`.
- [ ] The form handles `onsubmit` (not the button's click) with `event.preventDefault()`, so Enter saves and the page never reloads.
- [ ] An empty or too-long nickname (say, over 12 characters) shows a message in words in a `role="alert"` element that's on the page from the start, tied to the box by `aria-describedby`, with `aria-invalid` on the box, and focus goes back to the box.
- [ ] A `TeamSummary.svelte` imports the same party and shows each member's nickname (or name), and it changes the moment a nickname saves.
- [ ] No warnings in the browser console or the terminal.

## 3. Generate

Plan mode first: give Claude your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and ask for the plan before any code: what moves into `party.svelte.js`, which component owns the form, and where the error and the focus code live. Agree on it, then one step at a time, checking the page after each. When Claude leaves a `TODO(human)`, write it yourself.

**Watch for:** `export let party` (Svelte won't build it once anything reassigns it), the error `<p>` wrapped in `{#if}` so it only appears with the error (often not announced), `on:submit` or `createEventDispatcher` (Svelte 4), a click handler on the button instead of `onsubmit`, `aria-invalid` hard-coded to `true`, and the summary passed the party as a prop or given its own copy. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch's five rows on your own page:

- Type in the box. Does its label stay? Click the label: does the cursor jump into the box?
- Type a nickname and press Enter, not the button. Does it save without a reload?
- Save an empty nickname, then a 13-letter one. Do you get words, not only red? Turn on VoiceOver (Cmd+F5) and try again: does it read the label, "invalid", and the message?
- After each error, where's the cursor? Can you fix the nickname without touching the mouse?
- Save "Sparky" for Pikachu. Does the Team summary change at once? Search your components for a second `$state([` of the party: there shouldn't be one.
