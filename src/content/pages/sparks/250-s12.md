# S12 · Final Project audit

Add one bit of motion to your [Final Project](/250/final-project/) that has a reason to be there, with a calmer version for anyone who asks for reduced motion. Then audit the whole Final Project, write down what you found, and fix at least two things. Plan on 30–40 minutes (it's your Final Project time too). It's due before class on Mon Dec 7 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

This Spark works on your Final Project itself, not a copy of an old page; its own folder holds the audit table and `NOTES.md`. Nine days before the Final Project is due is the right time to find problems. The tools find some; your keyboard, 320px, and 200% zoom find the rest. The step most people miss is a hover effect with no matching `:focus-visible`, so keyboard users never see it. If the agent "fixes" a problem by deleting the content, say no.

## 1. Intent

Create `sparks/s12-motion-audit/` with a `NOTES.md` copied from `starter/NOTES.md`. In its **Intent** section, write two or three sentences: what the motion in your Final Project is *for* (what does it help someone notice or understand?), and who your audit is protecting. Name the person from your S09 intent brief.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your audit is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] Your Final Project has at least one transition or animation with a reason (say it in `NOTES.md`), added only inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` or with a calmer version for `reduce`.
- [ ] Nothing in your Final Project loops forever or flashes, and every hover effect also works on `:focus-visible`.
- [ ] `sparks/s12-motion-audit/index.html` is an audit table: a `<table>` with a `<caption>` and `<th scope="col">` headers, with one row per check: the check, how you ran it, what you found, and the fix (or your plan).
- [ ] The audit covers every page of your Final Project for: Feedback checks (validator and axe), keyboard only, 200% zoom, 320px, contrast spot checks, and a two-minute screen-reader pass.
- [ ] At least two problems are fixed in your Final Project, each linked from the table to its commit on GitHub.
- [ ] The findings are yours, in your own words.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

For the motion, give the agent your intent and ask for a plan first: what moves, for how long, and what happens with reduced motion on.

For the audit, the agent can build the empty table (`Make an accessible audit table with these columns`) and help you fix what you find. It can't do the audit: it can't press Tab on your live site, zoom it, or listen to it. When you ask it to fix something, ask what caused it first.

**Watch for:** `infinite` animations, motion with no reduced-motion version, hover effects missing on focus, and an agent that "fixes" a problem by deleting the content that had it. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Emulate `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` in DevTools and check your motion calms down or stops.
- Run every check in your table yourself, on the **live** site, not a local copy.
- After each fix, run the check again, and push; then read the **Feedback checks** Summary.
- Click each commit link in your table: does it open the fix?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on your reduced-motion rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand. The problems you haven't fixed yet are good **Open questions**.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s12-motion-audit` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
