# S12 · Motion and an audit

## The problem

A volunteer group's **Saturday cleanup** page looks finished on a laptop with a mouse, and the person who made it says it's done. It isn't. A banner **bounces forever**; the event cards light up **on hover only**, so keyboard users never see it, and the focus ring has been switched off; the "Sign up" control is something you **can't reach with Tab**; at **320px** the page scrolls sideways, and at **200% zoom** part of it is cut off; some grey text is hard to read, and an image says nothing to a screen reader. The validator and axe will find some of this. Your keyboard, a narrow window, zoom, and a screen reader find the rest.

Your job is the "Wanted" column: audit the page, write down what you found, fix it, and replace the endless bounce with one bit of motion that has a reason and calms down for anyone who asks for reduced motion. The page has **at least eight** problems, not all of them in the sketch; find and fix at least six. Plan on 20–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Dec 7 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

The tools find some problems; your keyboard, 320px, and 200% zoom find the rest. The step most people miss is a hover effect with no matching `:focus-visible`, so keyboard users never see it. If the agent "fixes" a problem by deleting the content, say no.

## 1. Intent

Create `sparks/s12-motion-audit/` with a `NOTES.md` copied from `starter/NOTES.md`. Download <a href="/sandbox/250-s12/audit-me.html" download="index.html">the cleanup page</a> into that folder as `index.html` (it's one file, with its CSS in a `<style>` block). In its **Intent** section, write two or three sentences: who comes to this page (a neighbour signing up on a phone, someone using a screen reader, someone who gets dizzy from motion), and what the one bit of motion you'll keep should help them notice.

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Before you run anything, look at the page and its CSS and guess which problems the tools (the validator and axe) will catch, and which only your hands will find. You'll check it in step 5.

## 3. Generate

The audit is yours: the agent can't press Tab on your live page, zoom it, or listen to it. It can build the empty table:

```text
Make sparks/s12-motion-audit/audit.html: an accessible audit table with a caption and four column headers: Check, How I ran it, What I found, Fix. Leave the rows empty for me. Link it from index.html.
```

Then run each check yourself and fill in the rows. When you ask the agent to fix something, ask what caused it first, then for the smallest change that fixes it. For the motion, give it your intent and ask for a plan first: what moves, for how long, and what happens with reduced motion on.

**Watch for:** `infinite` animations, motion with no reduced-motion version, hover effects missing on focus, a "button" fixed with `tabindex` and a click handler instead of becoming a real link or `<button>`, and an agent that "fixes" a problem by deleting the content that had it. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch's five rows, then look for the rest:

- Emulate `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` in DevTools. Does your motion calm down or stop?
- Put the mouse away. Tab through the whole page: can you reach Sign up, and see where you are the whole time?
- Device toolbar at 320px, then 200% zoom on a laptop: anything sideways or cut off?
- Run every check in your table again, on the **live** page, after your fixes. Then read the **Feedback checks** Summary.

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: which problems the validator and axe actually caught, which only your keyboard, zoom, 320px, and screen reader found, and where your guess was wrong.

Then finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on your reduced-motion rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand. Problems you found but couldn't fix are good **Open questions**.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s12-motion-audit` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
