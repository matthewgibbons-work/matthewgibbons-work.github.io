# S09 · Release intent brief

**Concept:** Intent and Spec for your final project: the two steps of the loop the agent can't do for you.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

Today you decide what your [Release](/250/release/) is, before anyone writes code. You'll write a one-page intent brief: who the site is for, what problem it solves for them, its pages, and the "done" list you'll check it against on Dec 12. It's the most important page you'll write this term, and the agent can't write it for you, any more than it can write your `NOTES.md`.

## 1. Intent

Create `sparks/s09-release-intent/` with a `NOTES.md` copied from `starter/NOTES.md`. Then decide: the **default** (expand the Saturday Morning Archive) or **your own** site for a real or realistic audience.

Write your answers in plain words first, anywhere (a text file, a notebook). Use these headings:

1. **Who it's for.** One real or realistic person. "My cousin, who's 12 and has never seen a cartoon older than she is" beats "people who like cartoons."
2. **Their problem.** What can they do with your site that they can't now?
3. **Pages.** Three to five, with one sentence each on what's there.
4. **"Done."** Eight to twelve lines you'll check the finished site against, each one checkable with a yes or a no, and at least three about accessibility.
5. **Units.** At least four from this term you'll use, and where.
6. **One risk.** What's most likely to go wrong, and what you'll do if it does.

If your own idea looks too big or too small for three weeks, I'll tell you by Monday.

## 3. Generate

Here the agent does only the markup. Paste your plain-text brief into the chat and ask:

```text
Turn this into a valid, accessible HTML page at sparks/s09-release-intent/index.html with the same headings. Don't change my wording. If anything is unclear, ask me instead of rewriting it.
```

If it offers to "improve" your brief, suggest an audience, or write your "done" list, say no. A better question to ask it: "Which of my 'done' lines would be hard to check, and why?" Then you fix them.

**Watch for:** your words quietly rewritten, "done" lines that can't be checked ("looks good", "is accessible", "works well"), and features that need JavaScript (search, logins, saving things), which `AGENTS.md` rules out.

## 4. Verify

- Read each "done" line aloud. Could someone else check it on your live site and answer yes or no? If not, rewrite it ("is accessible" becomes "every image has `alt` text that fits its purpose").
- Swap with a neighbour: can they say who your site is for and what "done" means, from your page alone?
- Run the validator on the live URL.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: why you chose this project, what (if anything) you kept or rejected from the agent, one thing you looked up on MDN, and what you're least sure about. That last one is a good first **Open question**.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s09-release-intent` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
