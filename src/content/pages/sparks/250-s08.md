# S08 · Responsive at 320px

## The problem

Most visitors open a page like yours on a phone. A page built on a laptop often looks finished there and falls apart at **320px wide**: the two columns get squeezed until the sidebar is a sliver one word wide, the table runs off the right edge so **the whole page scrolls sideways**, and the quick fix, `display: none` on the sidebar, takes information away from everyone on a phone.

Your job is the "Wanted" pair: a new page that's one column on a phone, brings the sidebar back when there's room, and keeps the table in its own scrolling box. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Wed Nov 18 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit. **Before Wednesday, read the [Final Project](/250/final-project/) brief** (out Mon Nov 16): Wednesday's Spark is your intent brief for it.

It's a new page, not a copy of S07, so all your attention goes on how it changes with the screen. It's half of P4, due Tue Nov 24. Test at 320px in DevTools' device toolbar, not just a narrow window. The step most people miss is hiding things: `display: none` on small screens takes content away from people on phones, so make it fit instead.

## 1. Intent

Create the new folder `sparks/s08-responsive/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to). Then write two or three sentences in its **Intent** section: who reads this page on a phone, and what they need first. "Someone on the bus checking which trail is open this afternoon" tells you what goes at the top. Pick a topic with a main section, a sidebar, and a table:

- a trail guide: trail descriptions, a sidebar with parking and conditions, and a table of distance, climb, and difficulty,
- a farmers market: what's in season, a sidebar with hours and location, and a table of vendors by day,
- a campus club fair: the clubs, a sidebar with how to sign up, and a table of meeting times and rooms,
- a band's tour: news, a sidebar with ticket links, and a table of dates, cities, and venues,
- the **Saturday Morning Archive**: this week's shows, a sidebar with what's new, and a table of the broadcast schedule,
- or your own idea. An S07 topic is fine, but the page and its words are new.

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Look at the sketch and the unit's At a glance table, and name the properties, not their values: which do you expect to make the page one column on a phone, bring the sidebar back on a wide screen, and keep the table from pushing the page sideways? A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s08-responsive/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and it's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark.
- [ ] The page has a main section, an `<aside>` sidebar, and a `<table>` with a `<caption>`, with real content for your topic.
- [ ] The base CSS (outside any media query) is one column.
- [ ] Every media query uses `min-width` in `em`, and the sidebar moves beside the main section at least once as the window widens.
- [ ] At 320px wide (DevTools device toolbar), nothing makes the whole page scroll sideways, and the same holds at every wider width, including just above your breakpoint.
- [ ] The table sits in its own scrolling box with `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"`, and a name (for example `aria-labelledby` pointing to its caption), and you can scroll it with the keyboard.
- [ ] Nothing is hidden on small screens that wide screens show.
- [ ] Images (if any) have `max-width: 100%` and `height: auto`.
- [ ] `NOTES.md`'s Intent has your `Prediction:` line, and under it a `What it used:` line comparing your guess with the code.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

**Optional extra (not graded):** if something on your page appears in both the main section and the sidebar (a card, a featured item), lay it out with a container query, as in the unit's Live demo step 6, so it goes side by side only when its own box has room. Say in `NOTES.md` why a media query couldn't do the same.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and ask it to propose a plan before it writes any HTML or CSS: the one-column base, and the width where the sidebar comes in. Ask it why that width. "Because tablets are 768px" is the wrong reason; "because that's where the one-column text gets too wide" is the right one.

**Watch for:** `max-width` media queries, breakpoints in `px`, `display: none` on the sidebar or table for small screens, the table left to push the page sideways, fixed widths left in place, and a plain `1fr` column beside the sidebar: just above the breakpoint the table holds it wide and the page scrolls sideways (`minmax(0, 1fr)` fixes it). If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch on your own page:

- Device toolbar at 320, then type your breakpoint's width in pixels, then about 100px more (dragging can skip the width that breaks). Does the sidebar move beside the main section, and does the page stay put on both sides of that width?
- At 320, does anything make the whole page scroll sideways? Tab to the table and scroll it with the arrow keys.
- On a laptop, zoom to 400%. You should get the same one-column layout as at 320.
- After you push, open the live page on your own phone, and read the **Feedback checks** Summary ([how to read it](/250/kit/#reading-your-checks)).

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: the properties the agent actually used for each part of the problem, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why. A wrong guess you can now explain is worth more than a right one you can't.

Then finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on your `@media` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s08-responsive` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
