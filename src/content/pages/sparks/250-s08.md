# S08 · Responsive at 320px

**Concept:** responsive design: mobile-first media queries in `em`, and reflow down to 320px.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

Last week's page layout was built for a laptop. Now you'll turn it around: one column as the base, for every screen, and a `min-width` media query that brings back the sidebar when there's room. The table gets its own scrolling box, so it never pushes the whole page sideways.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s07-grid/` folder to `sparks/s08-responsive/` (leave S07 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: who reads this page on a phone, and what they need first. "Someone on the bus checking which episode to watch tonight" tells you what goes at the top.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s08-responsive/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and S07 is unchanged.
- [ ] The base CSS (outside any media query) is one column.
- [ ] Every media query uses `min-width` in `em`, and the layout changes at least once as the window widens.
- [ ] At 320px wide (DevTools device toolbar), nothing makes the whole page scroll sideways.
- [ ] The table sits in its own scrolling box with `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"`, and a name (for example `aria-labelledby` pointing to its caption), and you can scroll it with the keyboard.
- [ ] Nothing is hidden on small screens that wide screens show.
- [ ] Images (if any) have `max-width: 100%` and `height: auto`.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first: the base layout, and the width where the second column comes in. Ask it why that width. "Because tablets are 768px" is the wrong reason; "because that's where the one-column text gets too wide" is the right one.

**Watch for:** `max-width` media queries, breakpoints in `px`, `display: none` on the sidebar or table for small screens, the table left to push the page sideways, and fixed widths left in place. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Device toolbar at 320, then drag it wider slowly. Where does the layout change? Does it look right on both sides of that width?
- At 320, Tab to the table and scroll it with the arrow keys.
- On a laptop, zoom to 400%. You should get the same one-column layout as at 320.
- After you push, open the live page on your own phone, and read the **Feedback checks** Summary.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on your `@media` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.
