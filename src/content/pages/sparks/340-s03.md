# S03 · Promise party

**Concept:** Promises and `Promise.all`: waiting for several things at once, and handling the one that fails.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

There's no network yet. You'll use the unit page's fake `loadPokemon`, which takes a second and fails for `missingno`, so you can see exactly what parallel loading saves and what a failure looks like. ## 3. Generate

This is the class's Try it together, done properly: a page instead of the console, your intent, and the "done" list. Plan mode first: give Claude your intent and the "done" list, and agree on the plan before it writes code. Ask for the one-at-a-time version first, time it, then ask it to switch to `Promise.all`. You'll understand the difference better having seen both. When it leaves a `TODO(human)` (the failure message is a likely one), write that part yourself.

**Watch for:** `await` inside a `for` loop when you asked for parallel loading, a `.catch()` that only calls `console.error`, and `.then()` nested inside `.then()`. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Open the live page with the console open. Wrap the load in `console.time('party')` and `console.timeEnd('party')`. Is the `Promise.all` version about 1 second?
- Add `missingno` to the party. Does the page say what went wrong? Is the console free of red "Uncaught" errors?
- Remove `missingno` again. Does the whole party come back?
