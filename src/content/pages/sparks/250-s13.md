# S13 · Big build, shipped

**Concept:** a whole site through the loop, from intent brief to verified deploy.
**Time:** built in class; 10–20 minutes to finish and check, and due before class.

Most of this Spark happened in class: the five checkpoints on the [Big live build](/250/units/big-build/) page. Now finish anything you didn't reach, ship it, and check the live site against the brief's "done" list, the way you'll check your Final Project on Friday.

## 1. Intent

The intent is on the unit page (the Rewatch Club, for families in the neighborhood). In `sparks/s13-big-build/NOTES.md`, write it in your own words, and add one sentence on which checkpoint taught you the most, and why.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your build is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s13-big-build/` has `index.html`, `schedule.html`, `signup.html`, `thanks.html`, and one shared `style.css`.
- [ ] Every line of the brief's "done" list on the unit page is true on the live site, or `NOTES.md` says which isn't and why.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for all four pages in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] You've Tabbed through every page on the live site, and submitted the form empty and with made-up details.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

If you didn't reach a checkpoint in class, run its prompt from the unit page now, and its gate before the next one. Keep each request to one checkpoint.

**Watch for:** the agent rewriting a page that already passed its gate (check the diff in Desktop's **Changes** tab before you commit), and anything from the unit page's "What went wrong" list.

## 4. Verify

- Go down the brief's "done" list on the **live** site, line by line.
- 320px, 200% zoom, two contrast pairs in each mode, and Tab through every page.
- Read the **Feedback checks** Summary.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN (the line you `/dig-deeper`ed in class is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s13-big-build` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it). Then do the same for your Final Project, with your own S09 list: it's due Sat Dec 12.
