# S13 · Big build, shipped

Finish the Rewatch Club site you started in class on the [Big live build](/250/units/big-build/) page, ship it, and check the live site against the brief's "done" list, line by line. Plan on 10–20 minutes. It's due before class on Wed Dec 9 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

Most of this Spark happened in class, at the five checkpoints. If you didn't reach one, run its prompt from the unit page now, and check its gate before the next. Then check your Final Project the same way, with your own S09 list: it's due Sat Dec 12. The step most people miss is the diff: look at Desktop's **Changes** tab before each commit, in case the agent rewrote a page that had already passed.

## 1. Intent

The intent is on the unit page (the Rewatch Club, for families in the neighborhood). In `sparks/s13-big-build/NOTES.md`, write it in your own words, and add one sentence on which checkpoint taught you the most, and why.
