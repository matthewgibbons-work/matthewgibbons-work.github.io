# S12 · Data island

## The problem

Visitors to your practice site's party page want to **do** something: filter the party by type, or look up a Pokémon to see whether it would fit. The page is plain HTML built from PokéAPI, which is why it loads fast and reads with JavaScript off. **Right now** there are two bad ways to add the interactive part: drop your Svelte component onto the page and you get **a picture of it with dead buttons**; turn the whole page into a Svelte app and you're back to **a blank page without JavaScript**, with every visitor downloading the whole thing and **fetching the party again** in the browser. And moving between pages is a hard **flash**.

Your job is the "Wanted" column, on your practice site: one Svelte island on the party page (a filter or sort over the party, or a PokéAPI lookup, made from one of your P3 or P4 components if you like), fed by data the page fetched at build time, and view transitions between pages. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Dec 7 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

The work goes in `sparks/astro-practice/`; this Spark's folder holds only its `NOTES.md`. Claude is on the **Explanatory** output style: read its `★ Insight`s about the directive it picks, and decide. The step most people miss is the directive itself: without one, the component renders and its buttons silently do nothing.

## 1. Intent

Create `sparks/s12-data-island/` with its own `NOTES.md`. In its **Intent**: which part of your party page needs to be interactive, and which parts are better as plain HTML? Why?

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Which `client:` directive do you expect to fit where your island sits (`load`, `idle`, or `visible`), and for each row of the sketch, what do you expect fixes it? A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

## 3. Generate

Plan mode first: give Claude your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and point it at your S09 form (or the P4 component you're reusing) as the pattern for labels and errors. Ask for the integration, then the component in `src/components/`, placed with no directive (check it's a dead picture), then the directive, then the props, then `<ClientRouter />`. Read each `★ Insight` about the directive: do you agree?

**Watch for:** no directive (silent dead buttons), `client:load` on everything, a function prop from the page that arrives as `null`, the whole page wrapped into one island, and the island's own `fetch` duplicating what the page already fetched at build time. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch's five rows on your own site:

- Click every control on the live page, keyboard only. Does each one do something?
- DevTools → Network, filter "JS": reload and scroll. When does the island's script arrive, and is it the only one?
- Turn JavaScript off and reload. What's left, and does it make sense?
- With the Network tab open, use the island. Does it re-fetch the party the page already had?
- Turn on Reduce motion (macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Display) and move between pages.

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: the directive Claude picked and what fixed each row, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why.

Then finish `NOTES.md`. Name your directive and the two you didn't choose, with a reason for each, and one piece of data you moved from the browser to build time (or the other way) and why. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on hydration is a good dig-deeper starting point.

Then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `sparks/s12-data-island` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
