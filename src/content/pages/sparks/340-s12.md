# S12 · Data island

Add the island your intent brief planned to one page of your Final Project (a search of your API for more objects like an example, a lookup for a term your reader might not know, a filter over your examples), fed by data the page fetched at build time, and add view transitions between pages. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Dec 7 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

This is your Final Project's interactive part. The work goes in `final-project/`; this Spark's folder holds only its `NOTES.md`. Claude is on the **Explanatory** output style: read its `★ Insight`s about the directive it picks, and decide. The step most people miss is the directive itself: without one, the component renders and its buttons silently do nothing.

## 1. Intent

In `sparks/s12-data-island/NOTES.md` **Intent**: which part of your Final Project needs to be interactive, and which parts are better as plain HTML? Why?

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `final-project/` has the Svelte integration (`npx astro add svelte`), and `astro.config.mjs` still has no `base`.
- [ ] One page has a Svelte island, with its components in `src/components/`, that works on the live site: every control does something.
- [ ] The page fetches data at build time (in its frontmatter) and passes it to the island as props. Only data crosses: no functions passed from the `.astro` page.
- [ ] The island does something in the browser that needs JavaScript: a search or lookup against your API, or a filter or sort over your examples.
- [ ] The `client:` directive suits where the island sits, and `NOTES.md` says why you chose it over the other two.
- [ ] Everything around the island is plain HTML: with JavaScript off, the page still shows its build-time content and says what the island is for.
- [ ] `<ClientRouter />` is in your layout's `<head>`, and page changes don't animate when Reduce motion is on.
- [ ] Zero warnings in the build and the browser console; the island's accessibility (labels, announced states, focus) is as good as your P4 form's.
- [ ] `sparks/s12-data-island/NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed and pushed, **Build and deploy** is green, and the island works on the live page.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

Plan mode first: give Claude the "done" list and point it at the island in your intent brief and at your P4 form as the pattern for labels and errors. Ask for the integration, then the component in `src/components/`, placed with no directive (check it's a dead picture), then the directive, then the props, then `<ClientRouter />`. Read each `★ Insight` about the directive: do you agree?

**Watch for:** no directive (silent dead buttons), `client:load` on everything, a function prop from the page that arrives as `null`, the whole page wrapped into one island, and the island's own `fetch` duplicating what the page already fetched at build time. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Click every control on the live page, keyboard only. Does each one do something?
- DevTools → Network, filter "JS": reload and scroll. When does the island's script arrive?
- Turn JavaScript off and reload. What's left, and does it make sense?
- Turn on Reduce motion (macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Display) and move between pages.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`. Name your directive and the two you didn't choose, with a reason for each, and one piece of data you moved from the browser to build time (or the other way) and why. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on hydration is a good dig-deeper starting point.

Then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `sparks/s12-data-island` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
