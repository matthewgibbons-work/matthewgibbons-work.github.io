# S02 · Episode Guide, re-run

Rebuild the Episode Guide you made by hand before the restart, with the agent doing the typing: your episodes in a real table, on a page with landmarks that passes the validator. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It was due before class on Mon Oct 26; you can still finish or rework it until Dec 9. It's graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

Because you already know this material, you can judge everything the agent writes; that's the point of a first full run of the loop on familiar ground. Write your Intent before you open the chat. The step most people miss is the validator: run it on the live page, and fix every error before you call it done.

## 1. Intent

Before you open the chat, write two or three sentences in your `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: what this page is for, and who would use it. "A guide to Season 1 of *Animaniacs* for someone deciding which episodes to watch" is more useful than "an episode guide."

If the agent offers to draft it for you, say no. It may hand you a paragraph to paste even though `AGENTS.md` says it mustn't. Ask it for questions instead: the Intent has to be yours.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s02-episode-guide-rerun/index.html` exists, with its CSS in a separate `.css` file in the same folder.
- [ ] The episodes are in a `<table>` with a `<caption>`, and header cells use `<th>` with `scope`.
- [ ] The page has `header`, `main`, and `footer` landmarks and one `<h1>`.
- [ ] Every image (if you use any) has `alt` text that fits its purpose.
- [ ] The page passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first. You can point it at your old version in `archive/episode-guide/` and ask it to rebuild or improve it. Keep the episode content your own: give it your episode list (titles and air dates). If you don't, it may fill the gaps with episodes and dates it found or made up, and they'll look just as real.

**Watch for:** agents often build tables without `<caption>` or `scope`, put the whole page in `<div>`s instead of landmarks, or add a CSS framework. `AGENTS.md` tells it not to, but check anyway. If you catch one of these, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Open the page locally, then the live URL after you push.
- Run the W3C validator on the live URL.
- Tab through the page with the keyboard. Can you see where focus is?
- Compare with your old version in `archive/`. What's better? What's worse?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on the table markup is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s02-episode-guide-rerun` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
