# S14 · Bridge: your first script

**Concept:** JavaScript and DOM events, through the agent, in the one folder where `AGENTS.md` allows a script.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio. Finish it in class if you can: today is the rework cutoff.

Your Episode Guide gets a **Watched** button for each episode, and a small script that flips it on and off. It's your first JavaScript, and it's the first step of DIGHT 340. The agent will write most of it; your job is the same as all term: say what it's for, check it works for everyone, and explain every line.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s02-episode-guide-rerun/` folder to `sparks/s14-bridge/` (leave S02 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: who would mark episodes as watched, and why. "Someone working through a whole season who loses track of where they are" is a reason for a button to exist.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s14-bridge/` has its own `index.html`, CSS file, and one `.js` file loaded with `<script src="…" defer>`. No JavaScript anywhere else in your repo.
- [ ] Each episode has a real `<button type="button">` that toggles "watched", with an `aria-pressed` that changes between `"false"` and `"true"`.
- [ ] The watched state shows in more than color (a word, or a ✓ added with CSS).
- [ ] It works with the keyboard: Tab to a button, then Enter or Space.
- [ ] The Console shows no errors, and the script uses `const` or `let` and `===`, with no libraries.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words, and **Dig deeper** explains the `addEventListener` line.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first: what goes in the HTML, the CSS, and the script. Then ask it to explain the script line by line before you accept it. If it can't explain a line in words you understand, ask again, or `/dig-deeper` it.

(The button won't remember anything after a reload. Saving it takes more JavaScript; that's a DIGHT 340 problem.)

**Watch for:** `<div>`s with click listeners, no `aria-pressed`, a script in `<head>` without `defer`, `var` or `==`, a library "to make it easier," and a script added to any other folder. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Click every button, then do it again with only the keyboard.
- Open DevTools' **Console** and reload: no red.
- With VoiceOver (Cmd + F5), does a button say "toggle button" and "pressed" after you press it?
- After you push, read the **Feedback checks** Summary, and check the live page.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why; in **Dig deeper**, what each part of the `addEventListener` line does (with an MDN link); and anything you still don't understand. That's a good list to bring to DIGHT 340.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s14-bridge` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
