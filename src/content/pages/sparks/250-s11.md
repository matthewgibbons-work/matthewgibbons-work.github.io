# S11 · Tokens and dark mode

**Concept:** custom properties, `prefers-color-scheme`, `:focus-visible`, and `:has()`.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio (then on to your Final Project), and due before class.

You'll take your S10 page and give its design a vocabulary: every color and spacing size becomes a named token. Then dark mode is just new values for the color tokens, with every pair checked again. If your Final Project would benefit, do the same there next; this Spark is the practice run.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s10-multimedia/` folder to `sparks/s11-modern-css/` (leave S10 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: who uses dark mode, and what they need from it. "Someone reading in bed who doesn't want a white screen in their face" is a real reader.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s11-modern-css/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and S10 is unchanged.
- [ ] Every color and spacing size is a custom property on `:root`, named by its job (`--accent`, `--space-m`), and used with `var()`. No hex values or spacing sizes typed directly into other rules.
- [ ] `color-scheme: light dark` on `:root`, and a `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block that changes only the color tokens.
- [ ] Every text/background pair passes AA in both modes. The pairs and both ratios are in `NOTES.md`.
- [ ] A `:focus-visible` style with at least 3:1 contrast against its background in both modes, and no `outline: none` without a replacement.
- [ ] At least one `:has()` rule that does something useful on this page, explained in `NOTES.md`.
- [ ] No JavaScript.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.

- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask for a plan first: the list of tokens it will create, with their light and dark values and the contrast ratio of each text pair. Then check the ratios yourself; the agent's numbers are a claim, as they were in S03.

**Watch for:** a JavaScript toggle button, colors written out twice instead of tokens, hex values left behind in other rules, dark-mode pairs under 4.5:1, and a focus outline that disappears in dark mode. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Change `--accent` to `lime` for a moment. Did everything you expected change? Anything that didn't is a leftover hard-coded color. (Change it back.)
- DevTools → **Rendering** → **Emulate CSS media feature prefers-color-scheme**: check light and dark. Check every pair in WebAIM.
- Tab through in both modes.
- Read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push. (It checks light mode only, so dark mode is up to you.)

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `var()` or your `:has()` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s11-modern-css` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
