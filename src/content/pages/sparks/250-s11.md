# S11 · Tokens and dark mode

## The problem

You're making a checklist page that people use at night: things to find, read, or do, ticked off as they go. The first draft works at noon and fails at midnight. It's a **white screen** in a dark room, form controls included. Every color and spacing size is **typed into its rule**, so changing the accent means hunting through the CSS. Its colors have **only ever been checked on white**. Someone added `outline: none`, so with the keyboard there's **no sign of where you are**. And **ticking an item changes nothing** you can see.

Your job is the "Wanted" column: a new checklist page that works as well at midnight as at noon. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Wed Dec 2 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

It's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark. Use the unit's [At a glance](/250/units/modern-css/#at-a-glance) table and Live demo as your reference. It's CSS only: a light/dark toggle button needs JavaScript, and `AGENTS.md` rules it out. The step most people miss is checking contrast again in dark mode, in WebAIM, including the links, the focus ring, and a ticked item (a dimmed item can drop under 4.5:1).

**Your Final Project's next milestone is due Wed Dec 2 too:** your three example pages drafted. It's ungraded, and it's on the [Final Project](/250/final-project/#milestones) brief's milestone map. If your site would benefit, give it tokens and a dark mode the same way next.

## 1. Intent

Create the new folder `sparks/s11-modern-css/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to). Then write two or three sentences in its **Intent** section: whose checklist it is, who uses it at night, and what they need from the page in the dark ("someone reading in bed who doesn't want a white screen in their face" is a real reader). Pick a page:

- a stargazing checklist for this month's sky: planets and constellations to find,
- a bedtime-story shelf for a family: the stories read this week,
- a night-shift worker's end-of-shift checklist,
- a **Saturday Morning Archive** late-night marathon: the episodes to watch, ticked as you go,
- or your own idea, for a real or realistic audience.

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Look at the sketch and the unit's At a glance table, and name the properties, rules, and selectors (not their values) you expect to fix each row of the problem. A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s11-modern-css/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and it's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark.
- [ ] A checklist of at least five items, each a checkbox with its own `<label>`.
- [ ] Every color and spacing size is a custom property on `:root`, named by its job (`--accent`, `--space-m`), and used with `var()`. No hex values or spacing sizes typed directly into other rules.
- [ ] `color-scheme: light dark` on `:root`, and a `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block that changes only the color tokens.
- [ ] Every text/background pair passes AA in both modes, ticked items included. The pairs and both ratios are in `NOTES.md`.
- [ ] A `:focus-visible` style with at least 3:1 contrast against its background in both modes, and no `outline: none` without a replacement.
- [ ] A `:has(:checked)` rule that makes a ticked item look done, in more than color alone (a line through, a "Done" mark, or a border).
- [ ] No JavaScript.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] `NOTES.md`'s Intent has your `Prediction:` line, and under it a `What it used:` line comparing your guess with the code.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and ask it to propose a plan before it writes any CSS. Then have it build the page.

```text
Here's my intent and my "done" list for sparks/s11-modern-css/. Before you write any code, list the tokens you'll create, colors and spacing, with each color's light and dark value and the contrast ratio of each text pair in both modes. No JavaScript. Wait for me to agree.
```

Then check its ratios yourself in WebAIM: the agent's numbers are a claim, as they were in S03.

**Watch for:** a JavaScript toggle button; colors written out twice, once per mode, instead of tokens; hex values or spacing sizes left behind in other rules; dark-mode pairs under 4.5:1; a ticked item shown only by a color change, or dimmed below 4.5:1; and a focus ring that disappears in dark mode. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch on your own page:

- DevTools → **Rendering** → **Emulate CSS media feature prefers-color-scheme**: check light and dark. Do the checkboxes switch too?
- Change `--accent` to `lime` for a moment. Did everything you expected change? Anything that didn't is a leftover typed-in color. (Change it back.) Then do the same with a spacing token.
- Check every pair in WebAIM, in both modes, including a ticked item.
- Tab through in both modes: can you always see where you are? Tick an item with Space: does it look done?
- Read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push. (It checks light mode only, so dark mode is up to you.)

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: the properties, rules, and selectors the agent actually used for each row of the problem, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why. A wrong guess you can now explain is worth more than a right one you can't.

Then finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `var()` or your `:has()` rule is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s11-modern-css` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
