# S05 · Box model, nav, and feedback checks

Update your course kit so every push runs feedback checks, then add a nav bar to your S04 page with links big enough to tap. Plan on about 10 minutes for the kit update, then 15–30 minutes for the nav. It's due before class on Wed Nov 4 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit. Do the kit update first: the rest of the term's Sparks expect it.

The feedback checks run the W3C validator and an axe accessibility scan every time you push. They never block anything; they're a second pair of eyes, and you read them in your repo's **Actions** tab. The nav is also half of P2's layout work. The step most people miss is measuring: a link that looks big enough is often text-sized, so hover each one in DevTools and write down its width × height.

## 0. Update your course kit

1. Download the starter kit again from the course site (same link as Spark 1) and unzip it. Move the `dight250-starter` folder into your repo.
2. In the agent's chat, paste:

   ```text
   My instructor asked me to update the course kit. Move AGENTS.md, .gitignore, and the .github folder from dight250-starter/ to the root of my repo, replacing the old ones. Don't touch starter/ or anything else. Then delete the dight250-starter/ folder and tell me what changed.
   ```

3. In GitHub Desktop's **Changes** tab you should see `AGENTS.md` and `.github/skills/check/SKILL.md` changed, and `.gitignore` and `.github/workflows/feedback.yml` new (or `.gitignore` changed, if you made one in S03). Commit them on their own with a summary like "Update course kit (Nov 2)", and push.
4. On GitHub, open your repo's **Actions** tab. A run called **Feedback checks** starts within a minute. When it's done, open it and read the **Summary**: one table for the validator, one for axe. Your archive is skipped on purpose.

The new `AGENTS.md` is stricter about `NOTES.md`: if you ask the agent to draft a section, it should say no and ask you questions instead.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s04-type-scale/` folder to `sparks/s05-box-model/` (leave S04 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: who uses this nav, and on what. "Someone browsing on a phone with one thumb" is a constraint the agent can design for.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

- [ ] The kit update is committed on its own, and your repo has `.gitignore` and `.github/workflows/feedback.yml` at the root.
- [ ] `sparks/s05-box-model/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and S04 is unchanged.
- [ ] The CSS sets `box-sizing: border-box` on every element, at the top of the file.

- [ ] Every nav link is at least 24 × 24 px, checked by hovering it in DevTools, and the size is in `NOTES.md`. Aim for 44 px tall.
- [ ] Padding, margin, and gap sizes are in `rem` or `em`, not `px`.
- [ ] Focus is visible on every link, and nothing overlaps at 200% zoom.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first. Ask it how tall each nav link will be, and how it worked that out. Then check it yourself: that's the point of this Spark.

**Watch for:** links with no padding (text-sized targets), padding on `inline` links that overlaps the page below, `width: 100%` plus padding with no `border-box`, a fixed `height` on the nav, and links in a `<div>` instead of a `<nav>` with a list. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Hover each nav link in DevTools' **Elements** panel and write down its width × height.
- In the **Computed** tab, look at one link's box model: which layer made it tall enough?
- Tab through the page, then zoom to 200%. Can you see focus? Does anything overlap?
- After you push, read the **Feedback checks** Summary in the Actions tab. Anything there for this folder?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `box-sizing` or `inline-block` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s05-box-model` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
