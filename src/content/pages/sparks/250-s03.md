# S03 · Theme palette

**Concept:** choosing colors you can read and write, and proving every pair is readable.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

You'll build a one-page **style tile**: a theme, four colors that fit it, and the proof they work. It's a new page, not another Episode Guide, so all your attention goes on color. The agent will happily pick colors. Your job is to check them, with numbers.

## 1. Intent

Pick a theme and write two or three sentences in the `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: the mood the colors should set, and who will read the page and where. "A lighthouse at dusk: calm and a little lonely, readable on a phone in the sun" gives the agent something to aim at. A theme can be:

- a Saturday-morning show or cartoon era (your palette can start P1),
- a place at a time of day (a lighthouse at dusk, a desert at noon),
- a flavor or a food (mint chocolate, a bakery at 6 am),
- a mood (a quiet library, a 90s arcade),
- or your own idea.

Create the new folder `sparks/s03-theme-palette/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to), and write your Intent there.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s03-theme-palette/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file.
- [ ] The page has a heading on a `linear-gradient()`, a short "about this theme" paragraph (3–4 sentences), and four palette cards: page background, text, accent, and highlight.
- [ ] Each card shows a swatch, the color's name, its role in words, and its value written three ways: hex, `rgb()`, and `hsl()`. The three match.
- [ ] The CSS file lists the palette once at the top, in one of those formats, with a comment naming each color.
- [ ] The page includes a paragraph of sample text, an underlined link, and a button, all in your palette.
- [ ] A contrast table on the page lists every text and background pair you use, its ratio from the [WebAIM contrast checker](https://webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker/), and a word for what it's good for: "Body text" or "Large text only".
- [ ] Every body text pair is at least 4.5:1, and every large text pair at least 3:1. The heading passes at the gradient's worst point.
- [ ] Focus outlines are visible on every background, and nothing relies on color alone: roles and results are written in words.
- [ ] The page passes the [W3C validator](https://validator.w3.org/nu/) with no errors.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

<!-- done:end -->

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list. Ask it to propose the four colors, with each role and its contrast ratios, before it writes any code. Then ask it to write each color in all three formats. The agent's numbers and conversions are claims, not results.

**Watch for:** ratios the agent states but didn't calculate, hex, `rgb()`, and `hsl()` values that describe different colors, links with `text-decoration: none`, focus outlines that disappear against a new background, and a heading that's readable at one end of the gradient only. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Check every text and background pair in the WebAIM checker, including the heading at the lightest point of your gradient. Make sure the table on the page shows what the checker says, not what the agent said.
- Find two palette colors in Chrome DevTools' **Styles** pane and Shift-click each swatch to cycle through the formats. Do the cards show the same values? A point or two of rounding is fine. A different color isn't.
- In DevTools, open **Rendering** (from the ⋮ menu → More tools) and set **Emulate vision deficiencies** to *Achromatopsia* (no color). Can you still tell each card's role, find the links, and read the table?
- Tab through the page. Can you see focus on every background?
- Run the W3C validator on the live URL.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `hsl()` or `linear-gradient()` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s03-theme-palette` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).

**Keep your palette.** P1 asks for a color scheme and type system on a new page, and this one is a head start. You'll still check every pair again on the new page. S04 will set the type on this page, so its "about this theme" paragraph matters.
