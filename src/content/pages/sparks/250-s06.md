# S06 · Flexbox header and cards

Make a new page laid out with Flexbox: the title and nav on one line, then a row of featured cards that wraps when there isn't room, each one jumping to its own section further down the page. Plan on 15–30 minutes after the kit update. It's due before class on Mon Nov 9 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

It's a new page, not a copy of S05, so all your attention goes on the layout. It's half of P2, which is due the next day (Tue Nov 10), so the cards you build here are good practice for P2's show cards. Keep the order you see the same as the order in the HTML: if you want newest first, move the HTML, not the CSS. The step most people miss is Tabbing through the cards: they can look right and still send focus the wrong way. Do the Nov 4 kit update first (step 0).

## 1. Intent

Create the new folder `sparks/s06-flexbox/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to). Then write two or three sentences in its **Intent** section: what the page is about, what the cards are for, and who they help. "A quick way in for someone who doesn't want to read the whole page" is a reason for a card to exist. The topic is up to you: your S05 theme is fine, but the page and its words are new.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

- [ ] The Nov 4 kit update is committed: your repo has `.course-kit-version` and the `/update-kit` skill.
- [ ] `sparks/s06-flexbox/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and it's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark.
- [ ] The header is a flex row: the title on one side and the nav on the other, and it wraps when there isn't room.
- [ ] The nav holds a list of at least three links (for example: your hub, your S05 page, your archive), laid out with `display: flex` and `gap` instead of `inline-block`. Every nav link is at least 24 × 24 px.
- [ ] A row of three to six featured cards uses Flexbox with `flex-wrap: wrap` and `gap`. Each card has a heading, a sentence, and a link to its own section further down the page (an `id` on the section, and `href="#that-id"`).
- [ ] Below the cards, a short section of new content for each card, each with a heading.
- [ ] Each card's link text is different, and says where it goes ("Jump to the 1987 season", not "Read more").
- [ ] No `order` and no `row-reverse` or `column-reverse`: the order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.
- [ ] At 200% zoom, the header and cards wrap instead of overlapping or overflowing.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan before it writes any HTML or CSS: the page's sections, which elements become flex containers, and in which direction. If you want a different order (newest first, say), ask it to change the HTML, not the CSS.

**Watch for:** `order` or a `-reverse` direction, no `flex-wrap` (cards that squash at 200%), fixed `px` widths on cards, margins on every card instead of `gap`, and identical "Read more" links. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Tab from the top of the page to the bottom. Does focus follow the order you see, every time?
- Click each card's link. Does the page jump to the right section?
- Zoom to 200% and make the window narrow. Does everything wrap?
- After you push, read the **Feedback checks** Summary for this folder.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `flex-wrap` or `align-items` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.
