# S06 · Flexbox header and cards

**Concept:** Flexbox for a row of boxes: `gap`, alignment, wrapping, and keeping the order you see the same as the order in the HTML.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

You'll take the page from S05 and lay it out with Flexbox: the title and nav on one line, and a row of featured-episode cards that wraps when there isn't room. It's half of P2, which is due the same day.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s05-box-model/` folder to `sparks/s06-flexbox/` (leave S05 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: what the cards are for, and who they help. "A quick way in for someone who doesn't want to read the whole table" is a reason for a card to exist.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s06-flexbox/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and S05 is unchanged.
- [ ] The header is a flex row: the title on one side and the nav on the other, and it wraps when there isn't room.
- [ ] The nav list uses `display: flex` with `gap` (instead of S05's `inline-block`), and every nav link is still at least 24 × 24 px.
- [ ] A row of featured-episode cards uses Flexbox with `flex-wrap: wrap` and `gap`. Each card has a heading and a link to that episode's row in the table (an `id` on the row, and `href="#that-id"`).
- [ ] Each card's link text is different, and says where it goes ("See De-Zanitized", not "Read more").
- [ ] No `order` and no `row-reverse` or `column-reverse`: the order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.
- [ ] At 200% zoom, the header and cards wrap instead of overlapping or overflowing.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first: which elements become flex containers, and in which direction. If you want a different order (newest first, say), ask it to change the HTML, not the CSS.

**Watch for:** `order` or a `-reverse` direction, no `flex-wrap` (cards that squash at 200%), fixed `px` widths on cards, margins on every card instead of `gap`, and identical "Read more" links. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Tab from the top of the page to the bottom. Does focus follow the order you see, every time?
- Click a card's link. Does the page jump to the right row in the table?
- Zoom to 200% and make the window narrow. Does everything wrap?
- After you push, read the **Feedback checks** Summary for this folder.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `flex-wrap` or `align-items` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.
