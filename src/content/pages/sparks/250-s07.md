# S07 · Grid layout

## The problem

A page for someone choosing between things often ends up as **one long column**: the header, then card after card, then the quick facts the visitor actually needs to decide (opening hours, distances, prices) buried at the bottom, then the footer. On a laptop, half the screen is empty, two cards are never side by side to compare, and the facts are six scrolls away from the cards they're about.

Your job is the page on the right: a new page where the facts sit beside the cards, and the cards fit as many columns as the screen allows. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Nov 16 (there's no class Wed Nov 11) and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

It's a new page, not a copy of S06, so all your attention goes on the layout. P3, due the next day (Tue Nov 17), is a bigger version of the same problem. For now the page layout only needs to work on a laptop, because S08 makes it work on a phone, but the cards have to fit any width already. The step most people miss is dragging the window narrow: a grid that looks finished on a wide screen can squash the cards or make the page scroll sideways.

## 1. Intent

Create the new folder `sparks/s07-grid/` with its own `NOTES.md` (copy `starter/NOTES.md`, or ask the agent to). Then write two or three sentences in its **Intent** section: what the page is about, who it's for, and which quick facts they need beside the cards. Any topic with a few things to choose between works:

- a farmers market's vendors, with the market's hours and location beside them,
- a trail guide: hikes near campus, with parking and trail conditions beside them,
- a campus club fair, with the date, the room, and how to sign up,
- a band's tour stops, with ticket prices and how to get there,
- a recipe collection, with a pantry list,
- or your own idea. Your S06 topic is fine, but the page and its words are new.

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. Look at the sketch above and the unit's At a glance table, and name the properties, not their values: which do you expect to turn the long column into header, main, sidebar, and footer? And which will let the cards sit side by side without you choosing how many? A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s07-grid/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and it's a new page, not a copy of an earlier Spark.
- [ ] The page layout uses `grid-template-areas` with at least `header`, `main`, `aside`, and `footer`, and each element is placed with `grid-area`.
- [ ] The quick facts are in an `<aside>` beside the cards, with real content for your topic.
- [ ] The cards use `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(…, 1fr))` and `gap`, with no fixed number of columns and no media queries.
- [ ] As the window narrows, the cards drop to fewer columns, down to one at 200% zoom on a laptop, without making the page scroll sideways. (Phone widths are S08's job.)
- [ ] The order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.
- [ ] At 200% zoom, nothing overlaps or gets cut off.
- [ ] `NOTES.md`'s Intent has your `Prediction:` line, and under it a `What it used:` line comparing your guess with the code.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and ask it to propose a plan before it writes any HTML or CSS: the area strings it will use, and which element goes in each. Read the strings before you agree: you should be able to sketch the page from them, and it should look like the "Wanted" side.

**Watch for:** a card minimum wider than the space beside the sidebar, so the page scrolls sideways at 200% zoom (`min()` inside `minmax()` fixes it); a fixed `repeat(3, 1fr)`, `grid-column: span 2` on a card (it forces sideways scrolling on a narrow window), areas drawn in a different order from the HTML, the facts in a `<div>` instead of an `<aside>`, and media queries for the cards. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- In DevTools' **Elements** panel, click the **grid** badge next to your page's grid container to draw the grid lines and area names on the page. Do they match your plan?
- Drag the window narrower, slowly. Count the card columns as they drop.
- Tab from top to bottom. Does focus follow what you see?
- Zoom to 200%, and read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push.

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: the properties the agent actually used for each part of the problem, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why. A wrong guess you can now explain is worth more than a right one you can't.

Then finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `minmax()` or `fr` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s07-grid` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
