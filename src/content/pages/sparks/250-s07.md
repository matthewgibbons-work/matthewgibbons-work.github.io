# S07 · Grid layout

Give your S06 page a real layout with Grid: a header across the top, the main content beside a sidebar, a footer, and featured cards that choose their own number of columns. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Mon Nov 16 (there's no class Wed Nov 11) and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

It's practice for P3, also due Mon Nov 16. For now the page layout only needs to work on a laptop, because S08 makes it work on a phone, but the cards have to fit any width already. The step most people miss is dragging the window narrow: a grid that looks finished on a wide screen can squash the cards or make the page scroll sideways.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s06-flexbox/` folder to `sparks/s07-grid/` (leave S06 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: what goes in the sidebar, and why someone would want it there. "Quick facts about the show, for someone deciding whether to watch" earns a sidebar its space.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s07-grid/` has its own `index.html` and CSS file, and S06 is unchanged.
- [ ] The page layout uses `grid-template-areas` with at least `header`, `main`, `aside`, and `footer`, and each element is placed with `grid-area`.
- [ ] The sidebar is an `<aside>` with real content (for example, quick facts about the show).
- [ ] The cards use `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(…, 1fr))` and `gap`, with no fixed number of columns and no media queries.
- [ ] As the window narrows, the cards drop to fewer columns, down to one, without making the page scroll sideways.
- [ ] The order you see is the order in the HTML, and Tab follows it.
- [ ] At 200% zoom, nothing overlaps or gets cut off.
- [ ] The **Feedback checks** run for your last push shows ✅ for this page in both tables, or `NOTES.md` says what it found and what you did about it.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed with a meaningful message, pushed, and the live page loads.

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first: the area strings it will use, and which element goes in each. Read the strings before you agree: you should be able to sketch the page from them.

**Watch for:** a fixed `repeat(3, 1fr)`, `grid-column: span 2` on a card (it forces sideways scrolling on a narrow window), areas drawn in a different order from the HTML, a sidebar in a `<div>`, and media queries for the cards. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- In DevTools' **Elements** panel, click the **grid** badge next to your `body` (or the cards' container) to draw the grid lines and area names on the page. Do they match your plan?
- Drag the window narrower, slowly. Count the card columns as they drop.
- Tab from top to bottom. Does focus follow what you see?
- Zoom to 200%, and read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `minmax()` or `fr` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.

Then run [`/check`](/250/skills/check/) `sparks/s07-grid` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
