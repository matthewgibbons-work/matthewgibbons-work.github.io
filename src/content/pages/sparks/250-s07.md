# S07 · Grid layout

**Concept:** Grid: named areas for the page, and `auto-fit` with `minmax()` for columns that fit the space.
**Time:** 15–30 minutes in studio, and due before class.

You'll take your S06 page and give it a real page layout: a header across the top, the main content beside a sidebar, and a footer, drawn with `grid-template-areas`. Then the featured cards become a grid that decides its own number of columns. Next week's S08 makes the whole layout work on a phone, so for now the page layout only needs to work on a laptop; the cards have to fit any width already.

## 1. Intent

Copy your `sparks/s06-flexbox/` folder to `sparks/s07-grid/` (leave S06 as it is). Then write two or three sentences in the new folder's `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: what goes in the sidebar, and why someone would want it there. "Quick facts about the show, for someone deciding whether to watch" earns a sidebar its space.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your page is done when:

## 3. Generate

Give the agent your intent and the "done" list, and ask it to propose a plan first: the area strings it will use, and which element goes in each. Read the strings before you agree: you should be able to sketch the page from them.

**Watch for:** a fixed `repeat(3, 1fr)`, `grid-column: span 2` on a card (it forces sideways scrolling on a narrow window), areas drawn in a different order from the HTML, a sidebar in a `<div>`, and media queries for the cards. If you catch one, it's a great entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- In DevTools' **Elements** panel, click the **grid** badge next to your `body` (or the cards' container) to draw the grid lines and area names on the page. Do they match your plan?
- Drag the window narrower, slowly. Count the card columns as they drop.
- Tab from top to bottom. Does focus follow what you see?
- Zoom to 200%, and read the **Feedback checks** Summary after you push.

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`: what you kept, changed, or rejected and why, one thing you looked up on MDN ([`/dig-deeper`](/250/skills/dig-deeper/) on `minmax()` or `fr` is a good start), and anything you still don't understand.
