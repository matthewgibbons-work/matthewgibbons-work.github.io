# S08 · Party components

Split your S07 party list into components, a `PartyList` that owns the party and a `PartyMember` for each Pokémon, without changing what anyone sees or hears. Plan on 15–30 minutes. It's due before class on Wed Nov 18 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit. **Before Wednesday, read the [Final Project](/340/final-project/) brief** (out Mon Nov 16): Wednesday's Spark is your intent brief for it.

The skill is in the seams: who owns the party, what each component is given, and how a button press in one component becomes a change in another. Claude is still on the **Learning** output style: when it leaves a `TODO(human)` (a callback, or where the focus code now lives), write it yourself. The step most people miss is focus after a remove: it has to move up to `PartyList`, which can see the other members.

## 1. Intent

Write two or three sentences in your `NOTES.md` **Intent** section: what's each component *for*, and who benefits from the split (you next week, a teammate, a future you coming back to it)?

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

<!-- done:start -->

- [ ] `sparks/s08-party-components/` starts as a copy of your S07 project (Svelte 5 + Vite, `base: './'`), and the page looks and works the same.
- [ ] `PartyList.svelte` owns the party (`$state`) and is the only component that changes it.
- [ ] `PartyMember.svelte` takes the Pokémon as a prop, and tells `PartyList` what happened through callback props (`onremove`, and one more of your choice, such as `onlevelup`). It never changes the party or the Pokémon itself.
- [ ] `PartyMember`'s props are typed in `<script lang="ts">` with an `interface Props`, and `NOTES.md` reads one line of it in plain words.
- [ ] A `Panel.svelte` layout component takes a `title` prop and `children`, and renders them in a `<section>` labelled by its heading (`aria-labelledby`, with an id from `$props.id()`).
- [ ] No `ownership_invalid_mutation` (or any other) warning in the browser console, and no warnings in the terminal.
- [ ] Focus after a remove still goes somewhere sensible.
- [ ] Your hub `index.html` links to it under Sparks.
- [ ] `NOTES.md` covers every section, in your own words.
- [ ] It's committed, pushed, and the live page loads at `…/dight340/sparks/s08-party-components/`.

## 3. Generate

Plan mode first: give Claude your intent and the "done" list, and ask for the plan as a list of components with each one's props, before any code. Agree on who owns what. Then one component at a time, checking the page still works after each. When Claude leaves a `TODO(human)`, write it yourself.

**Watch for:** a child changing data it was handed, `export let` or `createEventDispatcher` (Svelte 4), types of `any`, and focus code left in the child where it can't see the other members. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

- Side by side with your S07 page: same look, same keyboard behaviour, same names read out?
- Browser console open, press every button. Any warnings?
- In `PartyMember.svelte`, search for `splice`, `filter`, `++`, and `=` on a prop. Only callbacks?
- Hover a prop in your editor (or ask Claude): what type does it say?

## 5. Explain

Finish `NOTES.md`. Include one line of `interface Props` read aloud in plain words, and why the child calls a callback instead of changing the party. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on `$bindable` (and why you didn't need it) is a good dig-deeper starting point.
