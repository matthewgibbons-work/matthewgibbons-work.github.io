# S11 · Notes collection

## The problem

You've written a `NOTES.md` for every Spark and Prototype this term, and they're the best record of how you work. **Right now** someone who wants to read them (your instructor, a future employer, you in a year) has to open one GitHub folder at a time; a note with an **empty section** sits there and nobody notices; and putting them on your site would mean **a page written by hand** for every note, and another every time you add one.

Your job is the "Wanted" column, on your S10 practice site: a notes index, and a page for every Spark and Prototype you've done, checked by a schema that requires all five sections. Plan on 15–30 minutes, plus fixing any old notes the build rejects. It's due before class on Wed Dec 2 and graded Complete / Not yet straight from your repo, so there's nothing to submit.

Use the unit's [At a glance](/340/units/astro-collections/#at-a-glance) table and Live demo as your reference. If you skipped S10 (it was optional over Thanksgiving week), do its steps 0–3 first: this Spark adds to that practice site. The schema is a rule for your own writing, and the build tells you which old notes break it. Fix those in your words, not Claude's. The work goes in `sparks/astro-practice/`; this Spark's folder holds only its `NOTES.md`. The step most people miss: the loader reads *every* `NOTES.md`, including this Spark's, so the build fails until this one is complete too. Write it before you push.

## 1. Intent

Create `sparks/s11-notes-collection/` with its own `NOTES.md`. In its **Intent**: who will read your notes pages (your instructor, a future employer, you), and what should they get from them?

**Predict before you prompt.** Under your intent, add a line that starts `Prediction:`. For each row of the sketch, which part of a content collection (the loader, the schema, `getCollection()`, a dynamic route) do you expect to fix it? A guess is fine; you'll check it in step 5.

## 2. Spec: the "done" list

Your Spark is done when:

## 3. Generate

Plan mode first. Give Claude your intent and the "done" list (not your prediction: that's for you), and tell it you'll paste the loader yourself (from the [unit page](/340/units/astro-collections/)). Ask for the schema first, and build: let the build tell you which notes need fixing before you write any pages. Then the index, then `[id].astro`.

Claude mustn't write your old notes' missing sections (`AGENTS.md` says so, and it should refuse). It can tell you which ones are missing.

**Watch for:** an offer to add frontmatter to every `NOTES.md` (you don't need it), sections marked `.optional()`, an unsorted index, links without the base, and a second `<h1>` on note pages. If you catch one, it's a good entry for "Accepted / changed / rejected."

## 4. Verify

Walk the sketch's four rows on your own site:

- `npm run build` in `sparks/astro-practice/`: count the notes pages against your `sparks/` and `prototypes/` folders.
- Empty one section, build, read the error aloud. Does it name the note and the section? Put it back.
- Add a line to an old note and build again. Did its page change without you touching a page file?
- `npm run preview`: open the index and three notes with the keyboard. In order? Back link? One `<h1>` each (check the accessibility tree)?
- After you push: **Build and deploy** green, and the live notes pages load.

## 5. Explain

**Check your prediction.** Under your `Prediction:` line, add `What it used:`: which part of the collection actually fixed each row of the sketch, where your guess matched, where it didn't, and why.

Then finish `NOTES.md`. Read one line of your schema in plain words, and say which old note failed and what you added. [`/dig-deeper`](/340/skills/dig-deeper/) on `getStaticPaths()` and when it runs is a good dig-deeper starting point.

Then run [`/check`](/340/skills/check/) `sparks/s11-notes-collection` and paste in the "done" list above (the **Copy** button copies it).
