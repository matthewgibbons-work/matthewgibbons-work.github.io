The story

Dungeons & Dragons (1983, US): six kids ride an amusement-park dark ride straight into a fantasy realm. The Dungeon Master gives each one a magic weapon, which the wizard Venger wants for himself.

The Dungeon Master's recruits

  • Hank, the Ranger, with an energy bow
  • Eric, the Cavalier, with a shield
  • Diana, the Acrobat, with a staff
  • Presto, the Magician, with a hat
  • Sheila, the Thief, with a cloak of invisibility
  • Bobby, the Barbarian, with a club, and Uni the unicorn
