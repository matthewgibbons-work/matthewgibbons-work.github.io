Our mission

Between 1981 and 1987, Saturday mornings belonged to space odysseys, transforming trucks and magic totems. The Saturday Morning Archive collects, catalogues and celebrates those shows, one record at a time.

The archive is fictional. It exists so a web design class has something worth linking to.

Broadcast hours

The viewing room is open by appointment during these hours:

  • Saturday: 7:00 a.m. to noon, cereal encouraged
  • Sunday: closed for rewinding
  • Weekdays after school: 3:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.
