Saturday Cleanup at Riverside Park

Riverside Volunteers · every second Saturday · all ages welcome

Next cleanup: Saturday, 9:00 am. Bring a water bottle!

Gloves, bags, and grabbers are provided. Meet at the north pavilion by the playground. If it's raining hard at 7:00 am, we'll move to the following Saturday and post the change here and on the library notice board.
